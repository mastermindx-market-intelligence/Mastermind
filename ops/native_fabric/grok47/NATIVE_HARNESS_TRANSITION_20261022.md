## 1. The outcome and non-negotiable boundaries

Chris should start an authorised mission in Mastermind OS, see the selected principal and its account budget, inspect the operator/worker tree, and receive a verified useful result without opening a Claude or Codex desktop app to keep the job alive. A native orchestrator must use the existing Fabric for delegated labour, with durable Executive identities and Agent OS continuity. Desktop applications become optional clients, not hidden lifecycle dependencies.

The routing objective is accepted useful outcomes per total root cost and elapsed critical-path time, not cheapest token price, maximum utilisation, or number of agents. Grok through Cursor and Grok through Build are two harness/account routes to a model family, not inherently different intelligence tiers. Their allowances may be separate or shared depending on the actual plan. The user reports a legacy monthly Cursor allowance only 5% used and an exhausted weekly Grok allowance; this is a current human observation, not an authenticated metering receipt.

Completion requires: authorised root admission; actual native principal START; bounded heterogeneous children; independent review and repair; exact-parent result consumption; a useful result shown in the authenticated OS; preserved recovery across app closure and process interruption; no duplicate side effects or allowance double-counting. Passing source tests, QUEUED, a Slack message, a CI merge, a displayed answer and parent ACK remain separate facts.

No account linking, subscription migration, purchase, on-demand spend increase, authentication migration, bypass of a live denial, or new premium model grant is authorised merely by this plan. Do not copy credentials between provider homes. Do not remove source, role, resource, permission or process-ownership checks to make a canary green.

## 3. The routing policy to qualify

### 3.1 Keep five identities separate

Reuse existing model/provider/account/host records to carry: model family and version; reasoning effort; execution harness; account/allowance identity; organisational role. Examples are Grok 4.7/high through Build versus Grok 4.7/high through Cursor, paid from independently verified allowance scopes. An account may expose more than one harness; a harness may expose many model families. Never infer separate spend from separate product labels.

Keep requested model, CLI-selected model and provider-reported model as separate evidence. When the CLI cannot attest the served model, report it as unknown rather than copying the request into an attestation field. Explicit pinned requests must remain exact; do not silently turn an explicit 4.6 request into 4.7 or switch an already-started job.

### 3.2 Eligibility before economics

The existing router/broker remains the owner. Evaluate, in order: current assignment and resource scope; task complexity and required capability; provider/account enrollment and source readiness; host/tool/context support; quality evidence and independent-review constraints; quota/spend availability; then expiry, monthly pacing and queue delay. Urgency never manufactures capability or clears a failed gate.

| Work | Proposed normal preference | Expiring-capacity treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C0 mechanical and C1 routine bounded work | Qualified economical workers with good matching outcomes; no general frontier promotion | Prefer eligible capacity that would otherwise expire, without creating filler jobs |
| C2 difficult bounded work | Grok 4.7 is a primary candidate alongside adequately qualified full GLM/Qwen; avoid blanket Flash-first treatment for hard debugging/integration | An expiring full model may win on a matching demonstrated task class, not merely because it has spare tokens |
| C3 consequential judgement | Explicit witness and a qualified principal/reviewer; preserve Sol/Opus/Fable role and grant distinctions | Do not downgrade minimum quality to empty a quota pool |
| Domain operator | A qualified model plus an actually enrolled recursive tool/return path | Model availability does not imply child-dispatch permission |
| Final review | Reviewer independent of the author's underlying model-family risk where required | Cursor Grok reviewing Build Grok is not automatically independent |

Grok 4.7 versus Sol equivalence is a Chairman preference/hypothesis, not a benchmark established in this inspection. Use a frozen matched-task evaluation before encoding broad performance equivalence. Keep `high` as the initial candidate reasoning level; `xhigh` should require measured value on difficult cohorts. Fast service variants need explicit latency benefit rather than being an unexamined default.

### 3.3 Use monthly capacity without either starving or exhausting it

The policy should deliberately use available Cursor Grok for suitable hard tasks when weekly Grok is exhausted. Monthly reset cadence alone must not demote it behind less suitable models. Conversely, routine work should not consume scarce principal capacity merely because it is prepaid.

For each real allowance, obtain remaining included capacity, exact reset time, observed-at time, shared-scope identity, request/account caps and permitted overage. Prefer vendor observations; retain dated human observations as such. Cursor's legacy monthly reset follows its billing cycle, not necessarily the first day of the month. New shared-weekly plans require one shared allowance, not two counted budgets.

A starting pacing indicator is `(remaining included capacity - reserved critical capacity) / time until reset`, compared with forecast eligible demand. It is a ranking input, not permission or a new quota ledger. Reserve and urgency thresholds must be configurable under the current policy owner and evaluated before installation. Unknown, stale, missing or contradictory metering must not become invented urgency or unlimited spending.

For GLM/Qwen burn-down, first allocate naturally ready eligible tasks that would otherwise be done later. Do not generate unnecessary work. Do not transfer a STARTed attempt to another provider. Preserve existing pre-task-only quota continuation, original root and independent successor attempts. Authentication, permission, billing, provider safety and uncertain effects remain non-failover cases.

The existing `pool stats` report already exposes evaluated verdicts and admission refusals. Required feedback closure: retain source/model/account/request identity, task class and complexity, actual execution state, bounded result digest, independent verifier, first-pass verdict, repair/escalation chain, parent-consumption receipt, and measured usage when available. Record all through existing Runtime/Fabric/Agent OS owners. Do not infer actual dollars from placeholder price columns. Compare by useful accepted outcome and whole-root repair/review cost. Do not retroactively label old unknown models as 4.7.

Run frozen matched tasks across candidate model/harness pairs, with equivalent inputs and tool grants, blind independent adjudication, explicit failure taxonomy, and confidence intervals. Start with difficult debugging, cross-file integration, adversarial review, synthesis and routine control tasks. Use held-out tasks and separate same-task retry effects. Judge a model-family policy only after meaningful task-stratified evidence; retain human overrides as explicit decisions, not training labels masquerading as measured truth.

### Account, host and permission tuning

Separate account quota from host physical capacity. A free host does not create provider allowance, and ten processes using one subscription do not create ten budgets. Keep native desktop usage and Fabric usage within any actually shared caps; do not copy settings across parallel Claude accounts. Distinguish routing YAML concurrency, CLI-native concurrency and the authoritative broker's installed cap before changing any of them.

Promote source-readiness diagnostics into the existing placement/readiness owner so an absent pinned donor or incompatible object store is visible before a long worker reservation. Preserve immutable-source validation, lazy-fetch/network restrictions and original source identity. The mb MiniMax incident is not resolved by a model change. Repair requires the exact configured source root, complete pinned objects and original guard readback, then a separately authorised bounded consumer proof. Do not replay the failed census ID.

Reconcile outdated models and expiring campaigns in existing configuration: OpenCode promotional lifetime, historical MiniMax model cohorts, GLM dated urgency windows, approximate Cursor reset metadata, and stale Grok exhaustion markers. Each correction requires its current provider/account evidence; absence of telemetry is unknown, not permission to spend. Retain Go's currently qualified short-task envelope instead of extending its security lifetime to accommodate long work.

### Critical-path economics

Choose an operator only when coordination reduces total work or supplies necessary integration judgement. Avoid a compulsory Meta-CEO -> CEO -> operator -> worker relay for trivial tasks. Bound fanout by shared allowance, eligible hosts, root budget and the principal's result-review capacity. Admit ready siblings concurrently through the existing DAG owner; do not let an active child's polling starve independent ready work. Use bounded event consumption and the existing wake/return owner rather than spawning watchers for every child.

### Principal and leaf sessions are different execution contracts

One-shot leaves may use bounded headless CLI execution. Durable principals require exact provider session/thread identity, retained conversation state, installed skill/tool attestations, recoverable event cursors and governed continuation. The existing native Codex leaf command uses `--ephemeral`; simply naming that leaf an orchestrator would not supply persistent context. Keep leaf behaviour until a separate principal profile is qualified.

Official Codex app-server exposes exact thread/turn events, resume and approvals. Use the existing qualified adapter and version-pinned protocol, not an unauthenticated remote WebSocket listener. Current docs call app-server/WebSocket experimental; supported deployment status and exact version behaviour must be part of qualification. Do not replace the existing transport merely because a new SDK exists.

Claude headless/SDK can return a session ID and resume that exact session. Global `--continue` is unsafe for a multi-session service because it chooses the most recent session. An interactive desktop account, a CLI session and an Agent SDK integration are not interchangeable permission grants. Current Claude documentation says `--bare` skips OAuth/keychain and uses API credentials; it must not be introduced as a subscription-harness optimisation. Skills, hooks and MCP discovery must be consciously pinned and verified through the current provider-policy owner.

Cursor can run headless and resume sessions, but a model in its catalog is not recursive Fabric enrollment. #1706 reports only read tools exposed on its inspected Studio route and a live imported-hook refusal. Preserve that refusal. Root/child tools require a specifically enrolled Cursor client, not reuse of another account-bound Fabric service or copied capability.

### Root-conserving delegation and continuity

Every child retains the original root, actual immediate parent, exact scope, budget and independent run identity. Child creation consumes root-wide allowance atomically. A leaf receives no recursive grant; an operator receives only its qualified bounded grant. Model changes, expired quotas or session context pressure cannot manufacture new child authority. Reject cycles and over-depth graphs through the existing hierarchy owner.

A principal consumes a returned child's terminal result, verifies source and output, issues repair or accepts, and continues its own original mission. A tool result, browser display and parent consumption need separate receipts. Native interruptions retain exact provider session, workspace, Runtime epoch, process identity, pending effects and next action. Resume only after same-carrier reconciliation; cancellation needs actual cleanup proof before releasing the lease. Replacement roots are not recovery.

Agent OS should retain the concise mission frontier, accepted decisions, exact source references, unresolved evidence, durable child links and next action. Provider transcripts remain in their existing custody. A fresh principal reconstructs from those references rather than replaying all logs. The OS can show source age, capability limitations and unresolved effects without becoming their authority.

10. Missing credentials, spend-limit refusal, source mismatch, permission denial or uncertain effect: stop the affected attempt; no automatic reroute to evade it.
11. Provider rejects before any task effect and existing continuation evidence qualifies: allow only the incumbent bounded quota-continuation mechanism, with a new successor ID and original lineage.
12. Existing worker remains on its generation while a new release is selected: no live migration or retroactive historical model relabeling.
13. Low reported acceptance with tiny or biased samples: investigate, do not automatically demote; missing outcome and admission refusal stay separate.
14. Multiple accounts on one host and one account across hosts: enforce account/shared-allowance caps independently from physical host limits.
