# Mastermind Market Source Validation

## Work inside the current assignment

Reuse the verified Mastermind source pin and applicable repository instructions; do
not restart full bootstrap for each observation. This method supplies no runtime,
tool grant, release approval or trade authority. Keep denied actions denied and
EFFECT_UNKNOWN on the original operation and carrier. Continue independent safe
work instead of widening access or retrying an uncertain effect. Use existing
Executive/Fabric, Agent OS, source, browser and deployment owners, not replacements.

## Frame the claim and knowledge cutoff

State the precise proposition, intended consumer, instrument/entity, economic
meaning, horizon and as-of time with timezone. Separate event time, release time,
source observation time, ingestion time and decision time. Historical analysis
must use information available by the decision cutoff, not a later restatement.

Use the existing research workflow for acquisition and synthesis. This skill is
the evidence-quality specialist, not a new research database or market-data feed.
Read [validation cases](references/cases.md) for common failure patterns.

## Establish a usable source chain

1. Prefer the relevant original filing, issuer release, official economic release,
   exchange specification or authorized licensed data. News interpretation and
   vendor/model summaries can identify a hypothesis, not substitute for the
   underlying observation. Distinguish a source report from independent proof.
2. Bind instrument identity: exchange, currency, units, series, contract/expiry,
   share class, underlying and adjustment basis as applicable. A ticker alone can
   be ambiguous. Compare like definitions, windows and denominators.
3. Preserve document/record identity, source location, version/vintage and
   timestamps. Mark inaccessible, partial, stale and secondary evidence explicitly.
   Unknown is not zero; missing a source does not disprove the proposition.
4. Check actual versus consensus versus prior/revised prior, GAAP versus adjusted,
   fiscal versus calendar periods, rates versus levels, basis points versus
   percentages, annualization and nominal versus real measures.
5. Recompute important arithmetic with verified inputs and units. Retain missing
   values and null reasons. Do not average incompatible observations into false
   precision; reconcile scope or present both with limitations.
6. Trace repeated stories to their original source. Ten copies of one release are
   one origin. Seek the strongest credible contrary observation and state what
   would falsify the leading explanation.
7. Preserve rights: authorized retrieval does not imply redistribution rights.
   Avoid copying licensed raw datasets, private transcripts, secrets or entire
   proprietary corpora into skills, public queries or published evidence.

## Keep economics and evidence distinct

For credit claims distinguish bond yield/spread, CDS tenor/convention, quoted
versus executable price and liquidity. A widening spread is not proof of missed
payment or imminent default. For earnings separate booked revenue, cash flow,
backlog, financing obligations and management guidance. For options distinguish
trade/quote timestamps, volume versus open interest, publication lags, vendor
inference and assumptions about dealer positioning.

Label each conclusion as observation, reported claim, derived quantity, inference
or forecast. A plausible mechanism plus price reaction is not causal identification.
Do not attach numerical probabilities without a stated estimation/calibration
basis. Identify unresolved evidence that could materially reverse the conclusion.

## Produce a decision input

Return claim, as-of cutoff, instrument/units, primary support, conflicts,
calculations, missingness, evidence confidence with reasons, falsifier and a bounded
synthesis of what changed and why it matters. Link underlying sources rather than
only a model summary. Save through existing research/Agent OS owners when required.
Keep descriptive research and forecasts separate from validated signals; this
method never originates or sizes trades, arms live alerts or grants data rights.
