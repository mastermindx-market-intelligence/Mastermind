# Market-source validation cases

## Credit spread narrative
Input: a headline attributes an equity decline to a company's CDS widening.
Require the quoted CDS tenor, observation time, quote source/convention, comparable
history and a plausible economic link. Check bonds/financing disclosures where
available. Report a narrative hypothesis unless stronger causal evidence exists.
Do not conclude inability to repay solely from a spread move.

## Revised macro vintage
Input: an October report evaluates a July signal using revised August data.
The July decision could not know that vintage. Rebuild with the available July
release/vintage or label historical validity unproven. Preserve the later revision
as a correction, not a retroactive July feature.

## Options positioning
Input: an API labels yesterday's open interest as live intraday dealer exposure.
Separate observed data, publication lag, model assumptions and inference. Confirm
contract multiplier and expiry. Missing exposure is not zero exposure.

## No accessible primary source
Input: three articles repeat the same inaccessible analyst note.
Treat them as one secondary lineage, state the access limitation, and seek
independent public disclosures. Do not fabricate the note or imply three
independent confirmations. Do not send internal evidence to a public search.
