## Design hypotheses and falsifiers

- Shallow capable graphs should reduce coordination overhead on cohesive work. Falsifier: lower acceptance or larger repair/integration cost than the deeper baseline.
- Additional hierarchy should be justified by independent domains, context isolation, verification or permission boundaries. A relay-only node is not value.
- Cheap leaves are candidates, not a universal rule; hard long-horizon work may need a stronger executor.
- Model, provider/account, harness and role remain separate. BytePlus versus Z.AI can be a qualified capacity tie-breaker, never a substitute for live eligibility.
- Compare topology, model assignment and context packaging separately on representative tasks; include failed work and review/repair consumption. No numerical savings claim before measured evidence.
