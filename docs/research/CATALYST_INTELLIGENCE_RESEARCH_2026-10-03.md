## 1. Verdict and decisions

The original thesis is useful: Mastermind should compose its existing event, specialist, financial, options, company and theme systems into an intelligible catalyst product. Separating event truth, outcome probability, issuer economics, expectations and research priority is the right direction.

The earlier paper is **not sufficient as an execution contract**. It understates some implemented carriers, overstates some reusable infrastructure, confuses dated evidence with current production state, and leaves important statistical and temporal failure modes unresolved. Its long sequence also delays useful product delivery behind an unnecessarily broad infrastructure programme.

This reassessment makes seven concrete decisions.

1. **Compose existing owners before inventing shared storage.** Bio already owns operational feature, forecast, outcome and evaluation records. Theme Graph owns its identity/evidence lineage. Company Intelligence owns company-event identity and context. Eval OS/qledger and the experiment/validation machinery already exist. Market Memory's similarly named forward primitive is explicitly temporary and synthetic, while its production admissions are narrowly source-specific. A cross-sector view is required; another general event/forecast/control authority is not justified. [B01] [M01] [M02] [M03] [M04] [M05] [E05]

2. **Adopt the usable code, with explicit connection work.** Bio #6712 contains a real What Matters Next composer, worker integration seams, publisher/route code and tests. Its ordinary environment-driven worker does not pass the required owner inputs, so this is a substantial implementation awaiting connection and proof. Semiconductor, Energy and Resources likewise contain meaningful code but unbound or deliberately empty source adapters. File count is neither absence nor production readiness. [B02] [B03] [B04] [S01] [S02] [S03]

3. **Repair evidence integrity before promoting numerical claims.** Both Special Situations control-window repair candidates still allow promotion-bearing comparisons between independently filtered event and control cohorts. Their reported mean and HAC test also need a single declared estimand. The MedTech reference implementation leaks information through derived qualification states even when the future event's source field is hidden. These defects block adoption of the affected inference path, not every factual UI or source-intake task. [E01] [E02] [S04]

4. **Deliver an early factual product and start prospective capture in parallel.** The first useful release is one real Bio path and one Government Revenue or Special Situations path through source evidence, correct identity, a shared read model, a concise What Matters Next view, dossier and revision. Unqualified probabilities or economic values remain explicitly unavailable. Complete evaluation machinery is a gate for predictive claims, not for a truthful event browser. Prospective coverage starts as soon as each existing writer can legally preserve the relevant observations; waiting until the final phase would waste the observation period.

5. **Replace the linear inference chain with a dependency model.** An event's occurrence, timing and outcome influence company economics; market price response also depends on prior expectations, valuation, liquidity, market conditions and exposure. Options prices are evidence about priced distributions under assumptions. They do not directly identify physical event probability, direction, or a scalar fraction of the event already “priced in.” Research priority is an operational selection policy with no sizing authority. [X03] [X04]

6. **Separate operational completion from predictive qualification.** A complete research workflow can be deployed while some models abstain. An “alpha” or directional claim requires its own preregistered, out-of-sample and prospective evidence. W-ANT is a distinct hypothesis programme, not the existing generic Anticipation page; its current sequencing in Mastermind #122/#123 must be reconciled. Core product release can precede its result. Full programme closeout still needs an explicit, evidence-based W-ANT disposition; KEEP_SHADOW is an honest non-promotion result and must carry its remaining observation obligation. [E06]

7. **Make the experience answer the investor's question.** The default view should say what changed, why it matters to this company, what may happen next, the relevant window, and what evidence would change the assessment. Detailed probabilities, cohort diagnostics, source chronology and modelling assumptions belong behind progressive disclosure. Paper is a design and interaction-evidence surface; production browser acceptance belongs to the deployed Macro/Terminal application.

### 1.1 Product outcome

A user can discover a relevant catalyst, understand the company's role and plausible financial consequence, distinguish known facts from estimates and unavailable data, compare that context with Prophet and Options, follow revisions, and later see what actually happened. The system can do the same through versioned backend read models, with traceable source generations and typed missingness.

The product is not complete when a schema exists, a reference model passes tests, a collector runs once, or a mockup looks finished. It is complete at the release level declared in §12, with evidence from its real producer, publication, authorized reader and browser paths.

### 1.2 Scope retained from the earlier plan

The end-to-end programme includes Bio, Defense/Government Revenue, Special Situations, Semiconductor, Energy/Nuclear/Grid, Resources/Mining, MedTech, company/thesis and Prophet integration, Options expectation evidence, Theme Graph economic mechanisms, Event Studio, revision/alert handling and post-event learning. Earnings and existing generic Anticipation are reused as adjacent event/context capabilities; their ownership is not absorbed. W-ANT remains separately qualified.

No trade execution, model-originated position sizing, general identity rewrite, new provider router, new task scheduler or replacement organisational memory system is commissioned by this paper.

## 6. Temporal, identity, revision and rights contract

### 6.1 Distinct clocks

| Clock | Meaning | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Effective/event time | When a real-world fact applies or an event occurs. | May precede publication; never alone proves the system knew it. |
| Source publication/availability | First demonstrable release through the specified source/channel. | Preserve channel, precision and evidence; submission date is not public posting. |
| First observation/ingestion | When the incumbent system actually captured or observed the version. | Must not be rewritten earlier during backfill. |
| Decision/assessment cutoff | Latest information admissible to the particular output. | All participating source, identity, model and coverage state must qualify at this cutoff. |
| Forecast made-at | When the model or analyst actually issued the forecast. | Preserve the actual clock and cutoff; source/thesis dates cannot substitute. |
| Accepted registration | When the admitted prospective owner accepted the frozen forecast. | Generate the clock in the accepted registration path. It must precede the first scored outcome observation and satisfy the family maturity policy; never backdate it to made-at, source or thesis time. |
| Correction/supersession known-at | When the later version became known. | Append a revision; preserve original forecast/features and old as-known output. |
| Outcome observation/maturity | When the target can be labelled under its family policy. | Separate from the event time; immature/censored/unknown is not a negative or zero. |

Date-only evidence needs an explicit conservative interpretation from its source owner; do not silently turn it into midnight UTC precision. ### 6.2 Eligibility before computation

For a query at cutoff (t), select only source versions and identity/rights facts known by (t), then resolve the valid/effective interpretation under the owner contract. Never take today's latest view and merely hide fields whose timestamps exceed (t).

A decisive invariant is:

> Holding the admitted pre-cutoff evidence and the current authorized request/entitlement policy fixed, arbitrary changes to ineligible post-cutoff inputs cannot change the historical assessment returned by the system.

Apply it to values, relationship/materiality/coverage states, ranking, alert eligibility, counts, explanations and derived identifiers—not only raw source fields. Historical economic rights are cutoff-bound evidence; current permission to display the result is a separate gate. ### 6.3 Identity and dependence

An event-owner ID is separate from issuer, security, listing and ticker. Preserve asset/trial/submission IDs for Bio; programme/opportunity/award/modification/recipient IDs for GovRev; transaction/party/security roles for Special Situations; project/ownership/royalty/stream IDs for Resources.

Issuer identity can outlive a ticker; a ticker can be reused. A fund exposed to a target is not the target. Multiple filings about one deal are not independent deal outcomes. A trial, asset or government programme may influence several securities, which introduces dependence even after event deduplication.

Use owner-qualified identity witnesses and temporally valid relationship edges. Ambiguous, competing or incomplete mappings stay unresolved. Displaying a company label does not admit a security-level payoff or authorize a holdings join.

### 6.4 Corrections, publication and caches

Keep authoritative evidence and frozen forecasts immutable under existing writers. Derived current views may be rebuilt; old as-known views and forecast snapshots must remain reproducible. Store references to consumed owner generations and a content digest, rather than copying whole private source planes into a new Catalyst store.

Cache identity must include the meaningful cutoff, owner generations, schema/model version, identity generation and entitlement/rights context. A source correction invalidates affected current projections and prompts an auditable thesis/research revision; it does not rewrite what the system forecast previously.

Publication must distinguish unavailable, stale, withdrawn, unentitled and empty-but-successfully-checked. A publisher that ran recently over an old source generation must show both clocks. An outage must not produce “no catalysts” or erase a previously known negative-risk event.

### 6.5 Model and label leakage

Historical source filtering cannot erase information in a later-trained model's weights. Model-extracted historical features that could know an event's later result remain exploratory unless an appropriate contemporaneous model or independently justified leakage-resistant method is used. For factual extraction, require source-supported spans and deterministic identity/clock validation; this still does not make retrospective free-form inference a prospective forecast.

Rights are checked by existing source and publication owners for capture, retention, derived use, display and redistribution. Public discoverability is not a blanket license for every downstream use. Do not buy data, change subscriptions, obtain credentials or widen access through this paper alone.

### 9.7 Finite decision rules

Each experiment must name its useful effect/precision threshold, eligible information floor, uncertainty standard, practical loss/false-alert bound, evaluation schedule and stop rule **before** observing held-out outcomes. Choose these from the intended decision and feasible data; they are policy choices, not literature-derived performance facts.

- **PROMOTE_ADVISORY:** the declared advisory claim passes its empirical and operational gates; specify the exact claim and limits.
- **KEEP_SHADOW:** evidence is insufficient or observation is still maturing; no unqualified numerical claim reaches the product. Record the remaining information requirement and existing observation owner. Failing to reject a null hypothesis is not, by itself, evidence of no useful effect.
- **REJECT:** a predeclared futility/adverse-performance region is reached, adequate evidence excludes the required useful increment, or necessary data/identity/rights/label feasibility is not supportable. Preserve the evidence and remove the unsupported claim. Distinguish this result from an underpowered or immature study.
