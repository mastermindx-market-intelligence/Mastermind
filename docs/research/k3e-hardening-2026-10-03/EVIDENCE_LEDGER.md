## Primary external evidence checked in ordinary web browsing

These sources support specific design cautions and comparison targets. They do not establish that K3E produces alpha, that any commercial product is entitled for Mastermind, or that every historical empirical result survives current markets and realistic costs.

### W01 — Campbell and Shiller: cash flows and discount factors

[The Dividend-Price Ratio and Expectations of Future Dividends and Discount Factors, NBER Working Paper 2100](https://www.nber.org/papers/w2100), December 1986; later published in 1988.

The present-value relation jointly involves future dividends and discount factors. This supports the audit's identification warning: a price observation is not a unique reading of future EPS or revenue. It does not prescribe a uniquely correct inverse-valuation model for K3E.

### W02 — Heston, Jacobs and Kim: option pricing kernels

[The Pricing Kernel in Options, Federal Reserve FEDS](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/feds/the-pricing-kernel-in-options.htm), 2023.

The paper studies economically different pricing-kernel implications that option-price fit alone may not distinguish well. This supports keeping pricing-measure inference separate from physical-probability calibration and treating strong option fit as insufficient proof of a physical forecast.

### W03 — Cochrane: discount-rate variation

[Author research record: Discount Rates](https://www.johnhcochrane.com/research-all/discount-rates); [Journal of Finance publication](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/j.1540-6261.2011.01671.x), 2011.

Discount-rate variation is a central alternative to cash-flow-only interpretations of changing valuations. Use it to require rival explanations and sensitivity, not to assert that every security-level move has one known cause.

### W04 — Chan, Jegadeesh and Lakonishok: momentum and earnings information

[Momentum Strategies, NBER Working Paper 5375](https://www.nber.org/papers/w5375), 1995 working paper; Journal of Finance publication in 1996.

Historical earnings-surprise and return information motivate separate demanding comparators and tests of complementarity. This is not current-sample validation of K3E or evidence that a newly constructed response residual adds information beyond its inputs.

### W05 — McLean and Pontiff: out-of-sample and publication decay

[Does Academic Research Destroy Stock Return Predictability?](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/jofi.12365), Journal of Finance, 2016.

The study documents weaker predictor performance out of sample and after publication. It supports fresh, realistic evaluation rather than preserving attractive historical signals by reputation. It does not prove that every possible K3E hypothesis must fail.
