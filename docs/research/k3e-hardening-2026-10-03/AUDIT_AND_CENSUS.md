### A consequential detail: populated fields are much narrower than schema fields

Each of the seven observation types has 67,600 physical rows. Finite-value counts are: average 66,869; covering-analyst count 67,264; growth 66,319; high 66,869; low 66,869; median **zero**; year-ago 66,319. Removing the nine defective average measurements leaves 66,860 supported average measurements under the empty-coverage criterion. This agrees with the candidate's central-average/median aggregate, but makes its actual composition explicit: the usable central values are averages, not medians.

Consequently, the existing schema does not provide fresh medians, analyst-level forecast age, individual revision breadth, analyst skill weights, or a distribution of future outcomes. High/low can support an explicitly labeled forecast range where the other semantics qualify; it cannot be relabeled standard deviation or a calibrated probability distribution. Averaging successive aggregate consensus snapshots is not an analyst-age-weighted consensus.

## 6. How the project became stranded

The source-supported explanation is a stalled acceptance/dependency chain: the first two naturally disjoint collection batches could not exercise revision lineage; a real empty-consensus defect failed the source audit; repairs landed; the canonical ledger retained its August next action; EXP-1 and its successors remained gated. By October, data and natural witnesses had accumulated without a corresponding merged current ledger transition. The new Information→Price assignment now explicitly reuses K3E and supplies that missing candidate proof.

This is an inference from records, not proof of an individual's intent, a continuously absent worker, or the exact organizational cause of every week of delay. “Abandoned forever” and “nothing happened since August” are both unsupported.

Preserve K3E-0's owner boundaries, naming law, typed missingness, descriptive-first authority, no fused score and no duplicate plane. Extend CPL-1 only with explicit estimands and strong controls. Preserve PHASE-1 as an explained summary with printed components, not an autonomous action. Add separately named, owner-routed scenario and fundamental-distribution work only after the necessary amendment; do not use the old identifiers to conceal new authority.

Kill the unique-market-forecast interpretation of a reverse DCF, uncalibrated physical probabilities from option prices, fake analyst-level statistics, hidden dealer positioning inferred from unsigned open interest, universal K3E scoring, and direct unvalidated Prophet rank/gate/size influence. Defer cross-asset generalization and expensive latent models until a simple US-equity vertical proves useful.
