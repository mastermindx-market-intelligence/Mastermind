## Product ruling

Mastermind OS must feel like a company of persistent AI teammates working together, not a static Meta-CEO office and not a collection of unrelated chats. Meta-CEO is an always-on Hermes-backed teammate within the same team, with role-specific planning and orchestration views. Every teammate is directly addressable where the actual conversation owner permits it. Group conversations, shared work, project plans, issues/PRs, recurring routines, drafts and artifacts belong to one coherent workspace.

The top-level **Team** destination owns this company experience. Its views are Overview, Conversations, Work, Projects, Routines, Artifacts and People. The Meta-CEO shortcut opens that teammate's persistent Bot Chat; it does not create another office or another bot/session. Existing Code and Settings destinations retain their deep T3/Hermes capability surfaces. Existing Inbox remains the human attention/decision owner.

**Always-on means persistent identity plus an actual gateway/runtime wake path, not permanent inference, a green dot, or a browser tab kept open.** Present gateway health, worker activity, last observed heartbeat, waiting/scheduled/paused/offline states separately. The user reports a twelve-bot team; every displayed name/count/activity in the design is a labeled fixture until an authenticated dynamic roster proves it. Do not hard-code twelve as an enrollment rule.
