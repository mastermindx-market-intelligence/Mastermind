## 0. Mission and done condition

Build Issuer Inflection Intelligence end to end so Mastermind can answer, for supported issuers and historical cutoffs:

- what materially changed in the issuer's economic trajectory;
- when the change first became knowable;
- whether it is operating, accounting, expectations, financing, narrative/disclosure driven, or mixed;
- whether it is accelerating, decelerating, confirming, contradicting, reversing or unresolved;
- whether the change is issuer-specific or shared with peers/themes/macro;
- what evidence confirms and contradicts it;
- what mechanism could explain it;
- what would falsify the mechanism;
- what should be monitored next.

**Program DONE_WHEN:** multiple heterogeneous real issuers travel from canonical owner-native evidence through deterministic longitudinal state transitions into a production “What Changed?” experience and grounded Ask Mastermind/Neural Web context, with correction-safe PIT clocks, typed absence/NOT_EVALUABLE, evidence-for/evidence-against, mechanism/falsifiers, no duplicate truth plane, and feature-level prospective validation. Prophet remains shadow-only until individual features separately earn authority.

## 1. Non-negotiable architecture laws

1. FIF remains financial-fact/statement/revision owner.
2. Fundamental Forensics remains SEC source/discovery/correction owner.
3. Earnings/Event Intelligence remains event/guidance/transcript/Q&A owner.
4. Capital Structure remains financing-state owner.
5. Canonical issuer/security identity is referenced, never reimplemented.
6. No second metric registry, query kernel, evidence store, transcript store, peer graph, clock system, or lifecycle/control plane.
7. The new plane is derived and read-only over canonical owners.
8. Unknown is not zero. Missing is not neutral. NOT_EVALUABLE is a valid output.
9. Architecture is not runtime. Merged is not PROVEN_LIVE. One golden issuer is not broad coverage.
10. LLMs may synthesize accepted evidence but never originate deterministic financial truth.
11. No historical consensus/estimate feature until licensed PIT snapshots, rights and correction semantics are proven.
12. No scalar issuer inflection score. No rank/size/gate/trade authority at birth.
13. Corrections/revisions are first-class and must be able to supersede a prior derived transition without erasing historical knowability.
14. Every visible product claim resolves to canonical receipts or typed absence.

## 4. Golden case charter

The corpus must test failure, not just success.

- AAPL: first development issuer; accepted FIF + event evidence; known NOT_EVALUABLE cross-filing case.
- SaaS: KPI definition/RPO/bookings/deferred revenue.
- Semiconductor: inventory/channel/capacity/utilization.
- Industrial: orders/backlog/price-cost/lead-time normalization.
- Homebuilder: orders/cancellations/incentives/community count/rates.
- Financial institution: NIM/deposits/credit/provision logic; industrial WC logic must not be applied.
- Acquisition-heavy: organic vs acquired growth and perimeter.
- FPI/6-K: source/reporting cadence variation.
- Restatement/reclassification: correction safety.
- Guidance reversal: commitment-state transition.
- Financing/dilution stress: capital consequences.
- Turnaround/value trap: false recovery vs durable recovery.
- Durable reacceleration: positive case without rewarding generic growth.

Every case gets a stated failure it exists to test.

## 5. Required fail-closed behaviors

Return NOT_EVALUABLE or typed refusal for:
- period/basis mismatch;
- unresolved restatement/reclassification;
- acquisition perimeter discontinuity without supported bridge;
- segment/KPI definition change without mapping;
- missing seasonality comparator;
- missing licensed estimate history;
- current-consensus leakage into historical state;
- transcript role/source-format ambiguity;
- stale/unverifiable source;
- present-day peer membership used historically;
- correlated evidence counted as independent confirmations;
- upstream BUILT_NOT_PROVEN capability treated as live.
