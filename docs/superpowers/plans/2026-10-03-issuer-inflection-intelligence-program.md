## 1. Outcome, scope and completion law

Deliver one coherent issuer research workflow that answers, for an explicitly supported issuer and cutoff: **what changed, when the supporting evidence became available, what economic interpretation is justified, what contradicts it, what remains unknown, and what to monitor next**. The same canonical evidence and derived transition identities serve the user interface and machine consumers.

The program has three separate acceptance records:

- **Engineering completion:** owner-native inputs, deterministic comparisons, correction-safe longitudinal projection, supported-sector coverage, production UI, discovery/alerts and grounded machine consumers meet the contracted release scope and operational gates.
- **Descriptive usefulness:** a preregistered analyst task evaluation demonstrates useful incremental information, not merely attractive output or reliable abstention.
- **Predictive research disposition:** each registered candidate feature is classified as supported for further shadow, rejected, underpowered or blocked by a named data/rights dependency. Positive alpha is not required for an honest research completion. Live ranking, gating, sizing and trading remain separately prohibited unless individually promoted through existing authority.

An MVP or a golden fixture is not full program completion. Conversely, lack of licensed expectations data does not prevent shipping honestly scoped financial/event intelligence. W0 must freeze a release support matrix with **unconditional core**, **conditional external-data extensions** and **unsupported sectors/metrics**, including what the Chairman's requested full scope still owes. No later wave may silently move a required core capability into “conditional” to close the mission.

**Proposed commissioning floors, not empirical claims:** beta should include at least 12 real issuers across four business archetypes; a broader release at least 30 across six. These are coverage floors, not proof of scientific validity. Every admitted metric needs its own qualifying history; a list of tickers with mostly absent data does not count. Banking, FPI/6-K and other difficult formats must be demonstrated or explicitly remain unfinished requirements rather than being represented as covered. W0 may tighten these floors or record a reasoned alternative before implementation; lowering contracted scope after observing failures requires an explicit scope decision.

### 4.2 Numeric and comparability law

For a permitted positive baseline, growth is `100 * (current / prior - 1)`. A zero/negative or policy-defined near-zero denominator does not receive a fabricated percentage; return the owner-approved absolute change or typed refusal. Margin differences are percentage points or basis points, not percentages mislabeled as points. Incremental margin requires a meaningful revenue change and explicit conditioning, not division by a tiny denominator.

Year-over-year acceleration `g(t) - g(t-1)` requires the comparable observations needed for both growth rates: normally `x(t), x(t-4), x(t-1), x(t-5)` in a quarterly series. Two observed releases do not automatically supply those observations or a safe vintage bridge. A same-filing current/prior comparison is not the same question as comparing how a prior period was restated across later filings.

Match fiscal duration, instant/duration semantics, currency/unit/scale, consolidated/segment scope, metric definition, GAAP/adjusted basis, acquisitions/divestitures and revision identity. A 53-week year or quarter/YTD difference cannot be ignored. Working-capital ratios require matched average balances and flows; DPO based on COGS rather than purchases is an explicit proxy only when the owner permits it. Industrial DSO/DIO/DPO logic must not be applied to a bank. FCF and organic growth retain owner/source definitions.

### 4.3 Time and correction law

A derived claim's first supported time must respect the latest load-bearing required input, not the earliest attractive source. Preserve public-source time, actual system knowledge and emission separately. Date-only/ambiguous times retain bounds; no default midnight precision. Historical reconstruction using a later method is labeled as such and is not an actual historical alert.

A correction cannot mutate the prior as-known result. Append source-linked revision/supersession artifacts through the existing owner. “Corrected now” and “as known then” are explicit query modes, with method version and input set fixed. A rights revocation can require suppressing present display while preserving an allowed audit reference; it does not authorize retaining prohibited raw content.

### 4.4 Interpretation and evidence law

Identical underlying disclosure carried in a release, transcript summary and generated brief is one dependence group, not three independent confirmations. Accounting identities and derived ratios also carry dependencies. Conflicting values with different definitions are an unresolved basis mismatch, not automatically contradictory business evidence.

LLMs may propose cited hypotheses and explanation text after deterministic admission. They cannot supply missing metrics, classify source roles by speculation, certify comparability, infer consensus, set materiality truth, or grant authority. Model/version/prompt/source context and output are receipted. Historical model-based evaluations must account for potential pretraining knowledge of future outcomes; only prospective logging reliably prevents that class of contamination.
