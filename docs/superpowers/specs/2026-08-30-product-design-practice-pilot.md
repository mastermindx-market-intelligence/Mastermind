## 1. Problem and observable outcome

A generalist Sol can recover product intent and an engineering worker can implement it, yet the
result can still miss the professional work normally supplied by a high-end product team:

- user-task and workflow reconstruction;
- information architecture and content hierarchy;
- interaction and state design;
- visual composition and design-system coherence;
- financial data visualization and density judgment;
- accessibility and responsive behavior;
- design-to-code fidelity and craft review.

The observable outcome is not "a Designer agent exists." It is:

> A real Mastermind product surface is materially better than a comparable generalist build because
> professional design method produced tangible pre-build artifacts, a qualified independent selector
> chose the strongest direction, implementation followed a frozen rubric, and live-product evidence
> demonstrated superior task quality at acceptable cost.

A prose design plan, a polished screenshot, a Figma frame, a green frontend test and a merged PR are
all intermediate evidence. None alone satisfies this outcome.

## 2. Authority and professional boundaries

### 2.1 Parent Product / Program authority

The parent `PROGRAM_CEO` or `PROJECT_SOL` owns:

- product thesis and primary persona;
- user and machine outcome;
- commercial/data/moat constraints;
- company-level tradeoffs;
- cross-practice integration;
- final acceptance inside Chairman authority.

The Product Design practice may not narrow the product because a simpler screen is easier to design,
remove intelligence because minimalism is fashionable, replace real workflows with generic SaaS
patterns, or reinterpret engineering difficulty as product intent.

### 2.2 Product Design mandate

The Product Design Lead owns, inside the frozen parent outcome:

- experience architecture;
- task flow and information architecture;
- interaction design;
- state completeness;
- visual direction;
- design-system and data-visualization composition;
- implementation acceptance rubric;
- professional design judgment returned to the parent.

It does not receive merge, deployment, production, credential, runtime, lifecycle or Chairman
powers merely because it owns the design artifact.

### 2.3 Design Engineer mandate

The Design Engineer or frontend worker owns bounded implementation against an accepted design
freeze. It may make reversible technical decisions inside the rubric. It may not silently redesign,
remove promised states, change the information hierarchy, or substitute a generic component because
the frozen composition is difficult.

### 2.4 Design Auditor mandate

The Design Auditor is read-only with respect to the design and implementation under review. It
receives the original outcome, selected direction, acceptance rubric, relevant real-data fixtures and
live product evidence—not the builder's authority or self-assessment. It may approve, reject or
request bounded repair. It cannot implement its own findings on the reviewed carrier.

## 4. Required information and forbidden substitutions

### 4.1 Required inputs

The design responsibility receives the smallest sufficient current bundle containing:

- frozen Chairman outcome and parent product thesis;
- primary persona, primary task and machine/intelligence job;
- current live or faithfully rendered product at relevant breakpoints;
- real or representative data states, never lorem-ipsum-only composition;
- current component, token, type, chart and interaction conventions where they exist;
- data/contract/time/null/correction/provenance behavior;
- current user research, product analytics or observed pain where available;
- accessibility, rights, security and performance constraints;
- explicitly preserved capabilities and no-rebuild boundaries.

### 4.2 Forbidden substitutions

The following cannot stand in for professional evidence:

- a prestige persona prompt;
- generic design inspiration without the actual user job;
- a screenshot without interactive and failure states;
- a component library inventory without information architecture;
- builder preference presented as user evidence;
- current code structure presented as immutable product architecture;
- a design trend presented as Mastermind doctrine;
- synthetic elegance achieved by hiding uncertainty, provenance or data density.

### 4.3 Information separation

Divergent concept generators should produce an independent first pass before seeing siblings where
feasible. A blind or partially blind design critic may be withheld the candidate authorship, model
identity and builder rationale. The selector receives enough context to evaluate outcome fit but not
irrelevant prestige cues.

## 6. Artifact contract

A material redesign cannot pass directly from idea to code. At minimum the selected path produces:

### 6.1 Current-state experience audit

- primary and secondary user jobs;
- current journey and points of friction;
- existing strengths that must not regress;
- information hierarchy and interaction defects;
- missing, misleading or dead states;
- accessibility, responsiveness and data-density findings;
- evidence references and unknowns.

### 6.2 Target experience architecture

- target journey from entry to completed user job;
- content model and information architecture;
- machine/intelligence surfaces required by the workflow;
- navigation and progressive-disclosure model;
- source/provenance and correction behavior;
- action, confirmation and permission boundaries;
- loading, empty, partial, stale, error, permission-denied and effect-unknown states.

### 6.3 Divergent reference compositions

At least two materially different directions for consequential work. A color or corner-radius change
does not constitute a separate concept. Each direction states:

- core organizing idea;
- primary task flow;
- hierarchy and density strategy;
- visual/data encoding strategy;
- design-system implications;
- tradeoffs and failure risks;
- real-data composition at relevant breakpoints.

### 6.4 Selection packet

The selector returns:

```text
selected direction
pairwise preference evidence
rejected alternatives and why
preserved dissent / unresolved tradeoffs
required repairs before freeze
confidence and evidence limits
```

Indiscriminate synthesis of all concepts is forbidden. Integration is allowed only after selection
and must preserve the selected direction's coherent organizing idea.

### 6.5 Design freeze

The accepted freeze contains:

- canonical screen and flow compositions;
- breakpoint/state matrix;
- component/token/style mapping;
- interaction and transition behavior;
- content/copy constraints where load-bearing;
- data, null, time, correction and source behavior;
- accessibility and keyboard/focus expectations;
- performance-sensitive visual behavior;
- allowed implementation discretion;
- changes requiring return to Design Sol;
- deterministic and professional acceptance rubric.

### 6.6 Live implementation proof

- exact implementation identity;
- browser proof at relevant breakpoints;
- real-data and all promised failure states;
- interaction and keyboard proof where relevant;
- visual comparison against the freeze;
- accessibility and overflow checks;
- console/network/performance issues where material;
- Design Auditor verdict and bounded defects;
- production path proof, not only a local screenshot.

## 7. Initial Mastermind design doctrine hypotheses

The first pack may begin with the following hypotheses, explicitly labeled provisional:

1. **Workflow before decoration.** Visual novelty cannot compensate for a broken research or decision
   path.
2. **Institutional density, not generic minimalism.** Mastermind may be information-dense when the
   density improves comparison and time-to-insight; density must be structured, not cluttered.
3. **Truthful state is premium design.** Freshness, source, uncertainty, nulls, corrections, partial
   failure and effect uncertainty remain visible at the appropriate level.
4. **Real data before polish.** Composition uses realistic values, lengths, distributions and edge
   cases before visual acceptance.
5. **One coherent system.** Reuse existing components/tokens and extend them deliberately; do not
   create page-local visual languages.
6. **No generic AI/SaaS card wall.** Cards are used when they express a real object or grouping, not
   as the default answer to every hierarchy problem.
7. **Progressive disclosure without information destruction.** Executive summaries retain paths to
   exact evidence and deeper intelligence.
8. **The primary job wins.** Secondary metrics, novelty and internal system visibility do not crowd
   out the user's main task.
9. **Complete states are part of the design.** Loading, partial, empty, stale, error, denied,
   correcting and effect-unknown states are designed rather than appended after build.
10. **Implementation fidelity is a professional obligation.** A beautiful source artifact plus a
    visibly degraded product is not successful design.

These hypotheses must be validated against real Mastermind use and Chairman decisions. No single
preference silently becomes universal doctrine.

### 8.1 `PPF-DESIGN0` — source-only practice

The first pack is usable through source/context alone. It has no Figma or browser write authority and

### 8.2 `PPF-FIGMA0` — read-only design-system canary

A later exact capability profile may expose bounded read-only Figma/design-system inspection:

- exact approved file/library coordinate selected by the host/owner, not model free text;
- component, variable, style and frame census;
- no credentials, unrestricted team/file search or mutation;
- current generation and tool-schema attestation;
- no claim that Figma authentication grants company authority.

### 8.3 `PPF-FIGMA1` — sandboxed draft write

The first modifying design canary writes only to an isolated draft file/page or other reviewed
sandbox. It may create native design artifacts from the accepted design system but may not alter the
canonical production library, publish components, manage users, change organization settings or
replace a selected product direction without current authority.

Every write binds one operation, exact target generation and effect receipt. Ambiguity is
`EFFECT_UNKNOWN`; no blind retry or alternate file.

### 8.4 Design-to-code

Figma or another visual tool remains an artifact surface, not a lifecycle or memory owner. The Design
Engineer receives read access to the accepted artifact and bounded repository write access through a
separate exact capability profile. The Design Auditor remains read-only.

## 9. Selector and auditor qualification

A selector is qualified for the exact task only if its composition has:

- the selected Product Design practice generation;
- access to original outcome and evaluation rubric;
- enough visual/product context to compare candidates;
- no unresolved authorship conflict requiring independence;
- an exact model/surface/capability receipt;
- no missing tool or context falsely represented as available.

A selector may return `PRACTICE_SELECTOR_UNQUALIFIED` rather than forcing a choice.

An auditor is independent only if it is not the same action-authoritative responsibility that created
or implemented the artifact and does not inherit write authority over the reviewed target. For a
high-consequence flagship surface, prefer a different concrete session and, where empirically useful,
a different model/provider composition.

## 10. Evaluation contract

### 10.1 Deterministic gates

Examples, selected per surface:

- required states and breakpoints present;
- no unintentional horizontal/vertical overflow;
- component/token invariants;
- contrast, focus, keyboard and semantic structure checks;
- chart labeling and non-color-only encoding;
- real-data length and null/correction fixtures;
- console/network errors and broken interactions;
- design-to-code geometry/tolerance checks where appropriate.

Passing these gates means the artifact is testable and complete enough to review. It does not prove
good taste or product value.

### 10.2 Professional judgment

Use pairwise comparison and explicit evidence across:

- primary-task clarity;
- information architecture;
- visual hierarchy;
- comparative comprehension and data encoding;
- interaction coherence;
- state honesty and completeness;
- Mastermind brand/system fit;
- originality without novelty theater;
- responsive and accessible usability;
- implementation fidelity.

The evaluator must expose material disagreement and cannot compress every dimension into one
unexplained decimal score.

### 10.3 Outcome evaluation

Where instrumentation exists, compare:

- primary-task completion and error rate;
- time-to-insight or decision-relevant information;
- navigation/recovery burden;
- feature discovery and use;
- user/Chairman preference with rationale;
- support/rework incidents;
- implementation and future-maintenance cost.

Outcome absence is `PRACTICE_OUTCOME_UNMEASURED`, not proof of success.

## 11. Generalist-versus-specialist falsifier

### 11.1 Experiment arms

```text
Arm A  Generalist Project Sol -> builder -> ordinary functional review
Arm B  Generalist Project Sol -> builder -> independent Design Auditor
Arm C  Product Design Practice -> builder -> the same independent Design Auditor
Arm D  Product Design Practice + governed visual/Figma toolchain -> builder -> the same independent Design Auditor
```

Arm D is optional until its capability profile is separately proven. A->B estimates the audit
increment, B->C the professional-method increment, and C->D the toolchain increment. Arms use the
same frozen outcome, source state and target surface. Budgets and models should be comparable enough
to attribute the increment, with all material differences recorded. Where only A and C can be
afforded, the result may assess the combined specialist composition but cannot claim an isolated
audit-only or method-only effect.

"The same independent Design Auditor" means the same frozen evaluator composition and rubric, not
one stateful session that learns arm order. Evaluation uses fresh blinded evaluator sessions or
equivalent randomized blocked isolation, hidden arm labels, balanced order, a frozen rubric, fixed
repair-round budgets and the same target/base/fixtures.

### 11.2 Isolation

- candidate creators do not see sibling solutions before first pass;
- evaluator packets omit irrelevant model/persona prestige;
- implementation workers receive their arm's accepted artifact only;
- production/evaluation fixtures are shared;
- evaluator and Chairman rationale are recorded before outcome disclosure where practical;
- token, elapsed, tool and repair cost are measured;
- evaluator sessions are fresh or equivalently blocked so arm order cannot contaminate later scores;
- arm labels are hidden from the evaluator until scores are recorded;
- repair rounds are budgeted in advance and cannot silently expand one arm.

### 11.3 Primary verdict

The pilot passes only if the specialist arm materially improves the complete product outcome—not
merely screenshot preference—at an acceptable incremental cost. Evidence should include artifact
completeness, professional judgment, deterministic defects, implementation repairs and live-task
outcomes.

Possible verdicts:

```text
PILOT_SIGNAL
SPECIALIZATION_PROMOTED
AUDIT_INCREMENT_PROMOTED
METHOD_INCREMENT_PROMOTED
TOOLCHAIN_INCREMENT_PROMOTED
NO_MATERIAL_ADVANTAGE
INCONCLUSIVE / OUTCOME_UNMEASURED
```

`PILOT_SIGNAL` is the maximum claim after one representative surface. Broader practice or fleet
promotion requires repeated evidence across more than one representative surface/task class. A
single-user Chairman outcome is labeled accordingly and is not universal UX proof.
`NO_MATERIAL_ADVANTAGE` means the architecture remains available for other practices but Product
Design is not generalized by prestige. `INCONCLUSIVE` requires a named missing measurement, not an
optimistic success label.

## 12. First real target selection

`PPF-DESIGN1` chooses one real Mastermind surface only after current product and repository
archaeology. The target should be:

- used often enough to observe real value;
- professionally design-sensitive;
- bounded enough for parallel arms;
- supplied with representative real data and failure states;
- reversible and safe to canary;
- measurable through a primary task;
- not already owned by an overlapping active redesign carrier.

The target is selected at action time. This source law does not assume Watchlist, Control Room,
Dashboard or another surface is collision-free merely because it is illustrative.
