# MASTER COMMISSION — Build the Mastermind Hermes Design Director & Astra Pro Design Orchestration System

**Role:** Astra Pro — Principal Architect, Design Systems Director & Autonomous Implementation Lead

**Priority:** High

**Mission type:** End-to-end research, architecture, implementation, integration, testing and production readiness.

**Primary objective:** Establish an exceptionally capable, persistent Hermes Design Director that understands Mastermind's visual identity, possesses strong design judgment, can prepare extraordinary creative briefs for Astra Pro, can review actual Paper designs, and can coordinate the entire design-to-implementation process with minimal Chairman involvement.

## 1. Executive directive

We have discovered that Astra Pro sessions in ChatGPT produce exceptionally beautiful, sophisticated designs in Paper.

Rather than replacing Astra Pro with other models, we want to build a permanent Hermes-based Design Director that maximizes the effectiveness of those sessions.

The intended organization is:

- **Hermes Design Director:** Persistent creative director, product design strategist, coordinator and quality owner.
- **Astra Pro:** Principal visual designer responsible for exceptional creative execution in Paper.
- **Specialist model workers:** Design researchers, visual critics, UX auditors, component researchers and implementation verifiers.
- **Claude/Codex/engineering workers:** Transform accepted designs into working product software.

We want this system to be capable of producing world-class interfaces, especially for Mastermind Terminal, Executive OS, Agent OS and our other applications.

**Your assignment is not to produce only a proposal. You own the technical investigation, design, implementation, integration and verification of this capability.**

Operate continuously across useful authorized phases. Complete one phase, verify it, preserve the result, and advance the next phase. Do not stop merely because a document, checkpoint, PR or preliminary test was completed.

Use existing Mastermind infrastructure and open-source components wherever practical. Avoid unnecessary reinvention.

## 3. Audit our actual Paper designs and establish Mastermind Design DNA

This is one of the most important parts of the mission.

We need the Hermes Design Director to develop excellent taste grounded in examples, not merely generic design principles.

### A. Collect the reference corpus

Inventory available authorized Paper documents, screenshots and existing product designs.

Identify:

- The strongest Astra Pro designs we've created.
- Particularly sophisticated page compositions.
- Excellent typography and information hierarchy.
- Elegant dark-mode interfaces.
- Outstanding financial dashboards.
- Beautiful charts, tables and analytical layouts.
- Excellent navigation, panels, filters and detail views.
- Distinctive interactions and component systems.
- Designs rejected or superseded during iteration.
- Final accepted versions versus intermediate experiments.

Read actual Paper artifacts where access is permitted and visually inspect screenshots.

Do not invent visual descriptions from filenames.

Distinguish between designs explicitly approved by the Chairman, designs accepted by another authorized owner, and designs merely judged attractive by an AI.

If approval provenance is unknown, mark it unknown.

### B. Build a curated visual reference library

Create a durable, versioned **Mastermind Design DNA** library containing:

- Reference screenshots.
- Paper document and artboard identifiers.
- Source/version provenance.
- Approval classification.
- Design principles demonstrated.
- Important components and patterns.
- Why the design succeeds.
- Known shortcomings.
- Appropriate reuse contexts.
- Related implementation references.
- Superseded examples and negative lessons.

Make this library retrievable by the Hermes Design Director.

A new design assignment should automatically retrieve only the most relevant examples instead of loading the entire library into context.

Use existing organizational knowledge and source ownership. Do not build a competing memory or approval database.

### C. Learn our aesthetic preferences

Extract a coherent design identity from the evidence.

Pay particular attention to:

- Premium visual sophistication.
- Information-dense interfaces that remain elegant.
- Beautiful but operationally useful dashboards.
- Strong visual hierarchy.

- Professional financial visualization.
- Desktop and mobile responsiveness.

Do not impose a rigid template that makes every page look identical.

The design language must preserve creative freedom and encourage exceptionally strong new ideas.

Treat Design DNA as a living reference standard, not a permanent aesthetic ceiling.

## 7. Architect Astra Pro session orchestration

We reportedly have four personal ChatGPT Pro accounts with approximately 200 Pro-mode turns per week each.

Verify current allowances, ownership, plan entitlements, reset behavior and applicable account/platform rules rather than assuming these numbers are guaranteed or interchangeable.

The goal is to allow Hermes to coordinate design work with Astra Pro sessions efficiently.

### Desired workflow

**Step A — Design assignment**

A product initiative requests design work.

Hermes receives the objective, retrieves existing design references, examines relevant product context and creates an excellent Astra Pro brief.

**Step B — Session selection**

Determine whether an eligible and authorized Astra Pro session is available.

Assess actual usage entitlement, current workload, existing design ownership and concurrent work.

Do not share personal account credentials, circumvent usage limits, or use multiple accounts as a mechanism to evade platform restrictions.

**Step C — Dispatch**

Investigate whether Hermes can initiate or continue an Astra Pro task using a supported, authorized integration.

Hermes has browser capabilities, but browser access by itself does not prove permission to automate ChatGPT.

Examine current OpenAI terms, supported browser interactions, first-party integrations, Work features, available APIs and connector capabilities.

Implement the strongest permitted route.

Possible routes to evaluate:

1. Supported direct task/session invocation and result retrieval, where available.
2. Authorized account-owner-assisted web dispatch using a complete prepared prompt.
3. ChatGPT Work or another supported task mechanism.
4. Existing approved ChatGPT/Slack integrations.
5. API-based alternate workers for functions that cannot legally or technically use ChatGPT subscription sessions.

Do not build a headless ChatGPT-output scraper, credential-sharing mechanism, CAPTCHA bypass, stealth workaround or rate-limit evasion.

If no supported autonomous launch path exists, fully implement the independent design, review and handoff capabilities and provide the smallest possible human-assisted dispatch step.

Do not claim browser automation is production-ready without both technical proof and an acceptable authorization path.

**Step D — Paper creation**

Astra Pro receives the task and performs creative work in Paper.

Preserve the original authorized Paper document identity, scope and single-writer safeguards.

Other agents should not mutate the same active design target concurrently.

**Step E — Completion notification**

Investigate an event-driven completion mechanism.

Prefer a structured completion message sent through an authorized Slack action from the Pro session, or another supported completion event.

Test whether the actual Pro session has the necessary Slack write capability.

If supported, have Astra Pro report completion in the same authorized workstream/thread.

Do not assume that adding a sentence to the prompt creates an asynchronous callback capability.

**Step F — Fallback status checking**

Investigate a bounded fallback for jobs that do not emit a completion event.

Our initial idea is:

- First status check around 40–45 minutes.
- Second status check around 90 minutes if still unresolved.
- Escalation or later reconciliation if there is still no trustworthy result.

These are candidate timing values, not proof of job duration.

Use existing authorized scheduler, watcher and lifecycle owners.

Prefer checking an official task status or canonical Slack/Executive state. Do not scrape ChatGPT output through unsupported automation.

Avoid duplicated timers, duplicate job submissions, endless polling and concurrent modifications.

**Step G — Review**

Upon verified completion, Hermes obtains the result and supporting evidence through an authorized path.

It then examines the actual Paper output.

Hermes may review the result directly or assign bounded specialist reviews to suitable vision-capable models.

These reviews can evaluate aesthetic quality, usability, implementation requirements and fidelity to the brief.

**Step H — Creative iteration**

If the design needs refinement, Hermes formulates a targeted continuation brief.

Prefer continuing the original Astra Pro design conversation when appropriate.

Preserve its context, design references, previously accepted decisions and completed work.

Do not restart completed designs unnecessarily.

When a design passes review, deliver a formal handoff for implementation and preserve its provenance.

## 9. Design an intelligent review and iteration strategy

Research and compare these options:

**Approach A — Astra self-review**

Astra creates the design, inspects screenshots, reviews its work and iterates within the same session.

**Approach B — Hermes visual review**

Hermes reviews the result using its primary vision-capable model and determines whether further work is necessary.

**Approach C — Specialist independent review**

Hermes assigns a focused visual audit to Kimi or another qualified model, then synthesizes the findings.

**Approach D — Hybrid review**

Astra performs local self-review; Hermes conducts product and brand review; specialist workers examine specific risks or disputed visual decisions.

Evaluate quality, context continuity, latency, model costs, duplicate work and practical reliability.

My preference is a hybrid model, but establish the final policy through evidence.

Do not make every visual adjustment trigger a heavyweight committee review.

Avoid models arguing endlessly about subjective preferences.

Define useful thresholds for acceptance, targeted iteration and escalation.

A designer should have sufficient freedom to make creative decisions while preserving required functionality.
