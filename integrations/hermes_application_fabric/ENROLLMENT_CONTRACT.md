## Implemented model and pool constraints

The fixed v1 binding carries a concrete model and refuses strict_pool. AUTO v2
requires a null requested model and commits the original route envelope. V3
applies the host-authorized concrete model before original inspection and
binding, preserving exact model identity rather than converting it to AUTO.
V2/v3 strict_pool must match the original owner's complete singleton scope;
Hermes cannot narrow a broader pool list itself.

The original owner checks capability, model/pool entitlement and physical
account eligibility before ranking/reservation. Multiple models on one account
retain that account's real shared quota owner; listing aliases must not create
capacity. Multiple eligible accounts for one model remain separately owned.
Unknown headroom is not usable capacity. The consumer adds no router or ledger.

The qualified attended relation is fixed-only. The separate #2225 attended
AUTO/picker extension retains its own source and qualification custody. Neither
Secretary/Meta AUTO support nor the five-descriptor source patch implicitly
qualifies its new descriptor or lends it a role's grant.

## Provider-specific scope

The original Go attended route needs its actual account/product/endpoint,
APPLICATION entitlement, authoritative shared headroom and enforceable
included-plan/no-overage or applicable existing budget evidence. The original
C1 refusal remains with its legitimate provider recovery owner.

A different already-authorized included, prepaid or free account may establish
the separate Secretary first-account slice when its existing owner qualifies
the actual route and funding boundary. No new provisioning key or monthly/USD
ledger is implied. A metadata response, balance, worker grant or unknown cost
does not manufacture that qualification. Experiential retains its actual
explicit/manual-use conditions; it is not silently enabled as automatic overflow.

Official Z.AI GLM 5.3 remains a separate protocol/tool/usage/account qualification
through its existing provider owner. Discovered worker accounts do not prove
APPLICATION eligibility. The wider rollout does not gate an independently
qualified first Hermes-primary account.

## Attended acceptance owed

1. Exact installed source and admitted service/principal/workload/route receipts.
2. Real GLM Flash inference, native Hermes tool request/result round trip, and
   usage/account attribution from the original owner.
3. Overlapping admitted turns proving eligible physical-account selection,
   limits shared with worker traffic and no account overcommit.
4. Pre-dispatch refusal and post-dispatch unknown-effect cases proving no replay,
   account switch, new operation or premature lease release. A timeout or429
   alone never proves that another provider call is safe.
5. Original close/usage/settlement receipt and the ordinary CLI/gateway user path.
6. Separately qualified GLM 5.3 and any authorized Experiential extension under
   their own accepted product/account policies.

The general primary slice's concrete evidence is in
[HOST_INTEGRATION.md](HOST_INTEGRATION.md#acceptance-journey). Existing release,
