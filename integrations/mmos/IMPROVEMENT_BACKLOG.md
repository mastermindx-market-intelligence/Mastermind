## Proposed changes to the lane rules

Exact sentences for the principal to add or replace in `LANE_RULES_V4.md`. The
principal owns that file; this lane does not edit it.

1. To "Returns" (from row 17, DONE written before the fact):

   > Mark a step done only after the check that proves it has run and its output is
   > on disk. A ledger line that turns out wrong is superseded by an appended
   > correction line that names it; never edit or delete a line already written.

2. To "Returns" (from row 29, model not recorded):

   > Your first ledger line and your return state the model that drove the lane and
   > the model of every child you started.

3. To "Children" (from row 5, cheap attack missed the critical findings):

   > An attack round by a child is evidence about the defects it found, not about
   > the ones it missed. A diff on a critical surface — admission, credentials,
   > exactly-once, source authority — is accepted only after the independent review,
   > not on its own lane's attack round.

4. To "The shared git store" or "Returns" (from rows 1 and 14, outage losses and
   long-generation deaths):

   > Work in pushed steps: a first push within ten minutes of starting, then a
   > commit and push after every slice that passes its check, and at least every
   > thirty minutes.

5. To "The desktop" (from rows 4 and 19, the T3 CLI picking the wrong server):

   > The `t3` command line picks its server from its home directory and port
   > settings, never from an address inside your arguments. Set `T3CODE_HOME` and
   > `T3CODE_PORT` to the server you mean and confirm that server answers before
   > any pairing command. The stock `~/.t3/bin/t3` wraps the Chairman's app and
   > prints a warning line in front of its JSON when aimed at a newer home.
