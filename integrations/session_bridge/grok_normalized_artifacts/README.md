# Grok normalized artifact provenance

The process owner captures Grok JSON, while the parent retains its extracted text. The exporter previously compared these lengths and refused successful artifact delivery. For new artifact-producing Grok operations, the existing collector now hashes actual stdout byte chunks; the executor verifies capture and normalized bytes, and embeds a versioned identity/contract/manifest binding in the existing terminal receipt. The exporter validates that binding while retaining all raw capture, cleanup, lease, parent and artifact checks. No raw private reasoning is retained or printed.

Old runs without this original producer evidence remain on the legacy exact-byte path. This cannot reconstruct a missing historical anchor or authorize another export of a refused operation.

The support digest also resolves the new routing snapshot from the canonical controller policy path; a present-invalid co-located policy refuses. Grok priority now reaches eligible scored classes, preserves quality demotion, samples one dated quota pair, and limits its economic exception to known leaf classes. Ubuntu-only placement applies to the installed adapter path.

Qualification covers actual binary capture and producer-to-exporter joins, 21 integrity negatives, invalid UTF-8, original plaintext compatibility, controller/artifact support closure and invalid-policy presence. These source checks do not establish live artifact delivery.
