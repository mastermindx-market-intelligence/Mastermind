## 4. Public primary-source ledger

All entries below were checked on 2026-10-04. Public page content and indexed official descriptions are documentary evidence. No listed commercial product was purchased or sampled. Historical coverage means the described product/history, not necessarily subscriber-visible first-print vintages. Unknown terms remain unknown, even where a marketing page uses the word real-time.

### S01

**EIA release policy and petroleum.** [WPSR schedule](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/supply/weekly/schedule.php), [critical release index](https://ir.eia.gov/index.html), [weekly report page](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/supply/weekly/).

The schedule separates the usual Wednesday 10:30 Eastern data release from later report formats and holiday exceptions. The weekly page documents a September 2026 presentation change. Design consequence: retain endpoint/format identity, actual publication receipts and exception calendars; do not infer an XLS/API payload's availability from an unrelated page or scheduled clock. Exact historical endpoint bytes remain a qualification requirement.

### S02

**EIA Open Data.** [Official API and bulk catalog](https://www.eia.gov/opendata/).

Documents balancing-authority actual/forecast demand, generation/interchange, generator capability and sectoral electricity sales. Bulk updates are scheduled at 05:00 and 15:00 Eastern, with a stated completion interval after each start. Therefore a petroleum release timestamp cannot automatically date bulk delivery. Public API access is free subject to agency API and reuse terms. Actual versus forecast series and geographic granularity must be explicit. No firm-level AI-load attribution follows from a balancing-authority total.

### S03

**STB rail-service reports.** [Official reports and archive](https://www.stb.gov/reports-data/rail-service-data/).

The agency describes carrier submissions by Wednesday close of business and publication by Thursday close of business, with exceptions. Carrier-specific and consolidated workbooks are available; metric coverage and reporting rules vary through time. Initial indexed official retrieval supported this description; a later direct open timed out. No workbook schema or release-hash sample was validated in this commission. Admission requires the actual fields, period conventions, archive timing and parent-company crosswalk. A USDA reformat of STB is not an independent measurement family.

### S04

**ERCOT large loads.** [Integration process](https://www.ercot.com/services/rq/large-load-integration/), [September 9 verification notice](https://www.ercot.com/services/comm/mkt_notices/M-A090926-01), [September 21 visibility notice](https://www.ercot.com/services/comm/mkt_notices/M-C092126-01).

The conclusion is deliberately bounded: public forms/process descriptions are insufficient proof of a public company/project-level history. This is not a claim that every ERCOT operational dataset is private. No confidential submissions were accessed. Batch Zero status changes are not energization observations.

### S05

**LBNL Queued Up 2026.** [Official index](https://emp.lbl.gov/queues), [publication and scope](https://eta.lbl.gov/publications/queued-2026-edition-characteristics).

The 2026 edition covers requests through end-2025 and explicitly excludes load interconnection, distribution-connected and behind-the-meter projects. The described Excel includes project records and a codebook. Its data license is CC BY 4.0 with attribution to LBNL and GridTracker. These documentary facts establish an available generation/storage research source, not subscriber-history reconstruction or a repaired incumbent collector. Edition year and measurement year are separate fields.

### S06

**Federal Reserve G.17.** [Current release](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g17/current/), [March 16, 2026 archive](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g17/20260316/), [April 16, 2026 archive](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g17/20260416/default.htm).

Official archived releases expose preliminary/revised values and vintages. They provide a concrete fixture source for revision-safe research. Manufacturing and utility utilization series are different measures; aggregate output is not a firm's utilization. Archive availability does not establish intraday receipt timing for every delivery channel.

### S07

**ALFRED/FRED time semantics.** [Real-time periods](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/docs/api/fred/realtime_period.html), [observations API](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/docs/api/fred/series_observations.html).

Real-time-period parameters distinguish historical vintages from today's values. Date-granular vintage fields are not exact intraday release timestamps. Qualification is series-specific; an ALFRED-capable API does not mean every desired series has complete first-release history. Upstream data rights still apply.

### S08

**Census M3.** [Program](https://www.census.gov/manufacturing/m3/index.html), [historical series](https://www.census.gov/manufacturing/m3/historical/timeseries.html), [releases and data](https://www.census.gov/manufacturing/m3/data/index.html).

M3 orders, shipments and inventories are monetary values, not generic physical unit counts. Taxonomy, seasonal adjustment and revisions require versioning. The historical-series documentation notes discontinuation of the separate semiconductor category in 2010. M3 electrical-equipment aggregates cannot identify transformer-specific units, datacenter demand or a particular supplier without further evidence.

### S09

The program provides historical releases and annual revisions. Classification/population changes and nominal-dollar measurement matter. A long-running survey does not guarantee one homogeneous, first-print, machine-readable series across all historical regimes. Use the public industry baseline to diagnose private-panel coverage, not to manufacture company-level sales.
