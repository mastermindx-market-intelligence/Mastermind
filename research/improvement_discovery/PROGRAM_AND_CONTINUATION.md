## Sensing and idea genesis

Domain expectations should come from existing accepted contracts and user jobs, not
an ever-growing universal checklist. Distinguish missing capability, inadequate
resolution, untested hypothesis, stale knowledge, contradictory evidence, disconnected
consumer, manual workflow burden and unused existing capability. Do not classify a
candidate solely by its empty field count or by model confidence.

For important gaps, the cognition brief asks what would become possible, why the
limitation matters now, what current capability can be reused, what cheaper explanation
might account for it, and which bounded observation would discriminate alternatives.
Explore cross-domain analogies and changed external capabilities without letting novelty
or spare compute override evidence, current scope, budget or scarce review capacity.

Use explicit benefit/cost dimensions already supported by Chairman Cognition: user
value, dependency unlock, learning, Chairman labor reduction, time to evidence, cost,
coordination risk, irreversibility and scarce cognition. No universal hidden score.
Hold, simplify and retire are legitimate outcomes. A system that generates more
ideas than it can evaluate is not improving autonomously.

- Conflicting observations: reconcile, never majority-vote them into certainty.
- Accepted item plus new gap: reconcile its material invalidator; do not redo it.
- Existing proposal: attach and check actual custody/effects; do not infer START.
- Missing discovery input at Agenda: visibly unavailable, existing ranked items intact.
- Malformed input: fixed non-echoing error; no private payload in the public projection.
- Research field/path presence: structural coverage only, not semantic/production proof.
