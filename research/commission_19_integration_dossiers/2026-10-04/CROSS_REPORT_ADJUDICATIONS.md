## Common rules and domain exceptions

All interfaces distinguish source publication/availability, observation, durable recording, parsing, derivation and actual serving. A later reconstruction cannot acquire an earlier system-knowledge timestamp. Recorded-decision replay, operational-as-known, source-as-known reconstruction and latest-revised recomputation remain separate views. Corrections retain original lineage and invalidate affected derivations; unavailable data is not zero or neutral. Rights must be qualified for the named use.

Domain-specific clocks and denominators remain explicit: financial fiscal target/unit/basis; filing versus settlement/reporting period; broker snapshot and censoring; macro scheduled release, original/corrected actual and baseline vintage; queue cohort/status rather than load; event effective time versus knowledge time. These differences must not be hidden by a universal freshness, surprise or confidence score.
