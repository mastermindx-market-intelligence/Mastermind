## 7. Architectural consequence: strict admission, permissive unknowns

EAF should be **strict about whether a demand exists and who owns authority**, but **permissive about optional ranking factors being unknown**.

That means:

1. no explicit valid executive demand -> do not manufacture one from prose;
2. valid demand + unknown optional impact/burn/deadline -> keep the demand, preserve unknowns;
3. valid demand + unknown/conflicted load-bearing authority/identity/effect -> `RECONCILE_FIRST`;
4. valid demand + explicit safe owner/worker/placement progress -> `AUTONOMOUS_CONTINUE` rather than interruption;
5. valid demand + exact future typed wait -> `VALID_WAIT`;
6. valid demand + same canonical root as another demand -> exact fan-in, all receipts preserved.

This prevents the source-completeness problem from becoming either false urgency or false silence.
