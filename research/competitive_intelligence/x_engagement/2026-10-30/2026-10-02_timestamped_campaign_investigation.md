# October 2, 2026 — timestamped Equity Edge giveaway cluster (bounded verification)

**As-of investigation:** October 30, 2026. **Provenance:** one indexable third-party public X profile archive; original status hyperlinks recovered from that archive; timestamps decoded from X Snowflake-style status IDs; no authenticated X API response, enterprise contract, paid ledger, or company account access. **Scope:** October 1–30, 2026, with one Sep 30 quoted original explicitly marked outside the window.

**Machine-readable evidence:** [14-row event ledger](2026-10-02_timestamped_campaign_sample.csv) (2 company-original posts, 6 creator giveaways, 6 participant tag posts), one event per unique original post ID. The ledger is a **selected slice from one archived public profile**, not an exhaustive or independently sampled dataset.

## Findings that are actually supported

1. **Six distinct creator accounts published Equity Edge-related giveaway texts on October 2** between **13:41:06.386 and 16:25:03.337 UTC**, a 9836.951-second (163.95-minute) window. Each requires following company **@EquityEdgeUK** and pseudonymous, non-branded hub **@EquitySpace1**. Five posts also require following the author (exception: BouterFx has just the two account handles in the shown text).
2. **Five of six quote the same official-brand-handle offer** (status `2105378606864122233`, **2026-09-30 19:25:50.576 UTC**); the sixth, @MisauFx, quotes a separate payout-recap status (`2105945895015776505`, **October 2 at 09:00:02.611 UTC**). This is *one* base offer appearing in multiple distinct quoted posts, not six independent brand campaigns.
3. One visible, low-follower **participant account @FinancialF33756** posted **six tagged participation messages** on Oct 2 from **16:44:17.172 through 16:54:33.471 UTC** — 616.299 seconds (10.27 minutes). It mentions the six creator handles individually along with @EquityEdgeUK, @EquitySpace1 and a largely repeated friend-tag set. The mirror labels corresponding creator promotions as retweeted on this participant feed. The *retweet action timestamps* are **not independently available**, and these six status IDs do **not** establish native `in_reply_to_status_id` metadata.
4. Such a localized burst is compatible with a **common promotional template / official offer launch / giveaway-entry incentive**. It does not demonstrate a common operator, payment, company-owned X profiles, bots, fabricated end users, purchased engagements, real conversions, or unusual frequency **relative to an established baseline**.
5. [Equity Edge's first-party affiliate page](https://equityedge.io/affiliate) claims **up to 30% commissions** on referred purchases and lifetime attribution. It establishes that an incentive channel exists. It does **not** establish that any of these six specific authors received money or participated in the formal program.

## Why this sample changes the investigation

**Previously:** a web of required follows, affiliate codes and retweets, without event timing, could be overstated as evidence of a single managed farm.

**Now:** this one cited profile makes the *mechanism* demonstrably time-local — one brand offer, several copy-pattern creator giveaways, and a participant rapidly tagging several entry posts. This supports a **campaign-synchronized acquisition structure**, not the identity or authenticity of account operators. The repeated participant's ten-minute activity is consistent with normal giveaway hunting, scripted posting, or other hypotheses; it does not discriminate between them alone. Account bios describing other brand affiliations elsewhere make a shared Equity Edge login especially uncertain.

## Collection limitations and responsible use

- This is a **purposively selected example**, selected from a single participant who engages in giveaways. **Selection bias is high**. Six creators and six tagged messages are not a numerator with a valid 30-day total-post denominator.
- The posts' status IDs encode **publication times**, not the times of subsequent retweets or follow actions. The mirror may repeat a quoted parent within multiple distinct page blocks; deduplicate by status ID.
- A post tagged with another account's handle is not automatically a native reply or reciprocal interaction. Avoid treating a mandatory follow **instruction** as a verified completed follow.
- First-party website descriptions are company claims (e.g., payout sums) and are **not verified payout audits**; reported `3x10K` or `40x10K` figures refer to simulated trading account size, **not cash awards**.
- No unconsented export, personal contact details, doxxing, claims of criminal wrongdoing, accusations of specific paid undisclosed promotion, or attribution to Mastermind competitors are made.
- This evidence is from a prop-firm **adjacent** acquisition niche. It is evidence about distribution, not a direct substitute for stock-recommendation platform customer lifetime value, conversion or research credibility.
