## 1. Executive finding

The prior Equity Edge growth study concentrated on **follow/repost giveaway instructions** and their visible participation. A separate public-source pattern now shows three cross-brand, self-declared affiliate creators inserting Hola Prime recommendations into **other users' rankings, product comparisons and competitor discussions**. Profiles publicly display affiliate/promotion relationships. Some recommendations repeat highly similar independently-reviewed-payout talking points; others **conflate a company-reported 33:48 average with a Deloitte-reviewed one-hour processing rate**, or use the word “audit” where the **firm's own primary FAQ expressly says no audit or assurance occurred**.

**This is evidence of a disclosed-partnership, creator-driven reply-amplification channel and a material accuracy risk in claim propagation.** Similar language may originate from a shared publicly available press release, advertising brief, news story, or ordinary creators reading one company source. **No common account controller, centrally issued script, paid per-reply instruction, bot, 30-day frequency, ROI, or intent to deceive was established.**

## 3. Audit of the repeatedly circulated payout claim against original disclosure

### First-party published claims and exact scope

- **Hola Prime’s independent-review FAQ:** https://holaprime.com/review-by-deloitte/ expressly answers whether it was an audit or certification: **No; it did not constitute audit or assurance**. The external engagement examined processing timelines and payout-denial absence over an October 15, 2025 to March 15, 2026 review window. The firm reports **98.35% of eligible processing events within one hour, 1.65% above one hour, zero denials within the defined qualifying payout population**. It says processing time starts at *receipt of complete and accurate information*, ending at *system approval*, **not necessarily at initial user action or actual funds receipt**. This webpage itself is currently **indexed, but direct full-page retrieval returns 403**; do not claim the engagement report is public. The FAQ says the detailed report is not publicly distributed and an official summary can be requested.
- **Official metric separation (July 6, 2026):** https://holaprime.com/blogs/prop-trading/deloitte-review-confirms-hola-primes-1-hour-payouts-and-zero-denials/ explicitly marks **~33 min 48 sec AVERAGE as COMPANY-REPORTED**, not independently reviewed; fastest 3m37s, average amount ~$4500 and $3.2M cumulative in that publication are also company figures. Only the 98.35%, 1.65%, zero eligible denials in the defined period are described as externally reviewed. **Search-indexed first-party source; direct fetch blocked 403**, so record this as indexed official text, not a full source read.
- **Independent original journalism (April 27):** https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/analysis/hola-prime-brings-in-deloitte-to-audit-payouts-as-trust-gap-widens-across-prop-firms/ says reporter inspected documents and clearly differentiates eligible/pre-approved funded traders from the firm's total customer base. Reporter uses “audit” in headline, whereas the firm's own narrower FAQ says **not audit/assurance**. This is a **source-language disagreement**; the precise assurance classification follows the more explicit FAQ. The reporter describes 33:48 as Hola Prime's self-reported operations.
- **Independent reviewer (July 31):** https://theindustryspread.com/hola-prime-review-1-hour-payout-deloitte/ emphasizes **zero denials applies to eligible preapproved requests**; trader rule enforcement and accounts stopped before the eligible stage lie outside its denominator.

## 4. Commercial incentive: public program confirmed, individual compensation not confirmed

Two publicly active Hola Prime program descriptions differ materially:

- **Futures affiliate FAQ:** https://holaprime.com/futures/faq/affiliate-program/affiliate-commission-framework/ states a **flat 30% on first purchase per new customer**, not repeat purchases. The [application FAQ](https://holaprime.com/futures/faq/affiliate-program/affiliate-program-overview/) asks for Twitter/social handles and states prior trading experience or a challenge purchase isn't necessary. The [commission conditions](https://holaprime.com/futures/faq/affiliate-program/conditions-for-earning-affiliate-commissions/) say minimum payout **$100**, paid every 30 days under conditions.
- **General marketing program landing page:** https://holaprime.com/best-prop-firm-affiliate-program/ advertises **10% / 15% / 20%** tiered revenue commissions, **bi-weekly payouts**, and a **$80 threshold**. These conflict *if considered a single identical program*. Because the former is explicitly within `/futures/` while the latter may concern another product/channel, an edition, or a separate offer, **do not assert universal inconsistency or compute a creator's pay from either page**. Record as unresolved version/product distinction; only individual program documents, enrollment date, invoices or direct creator statements could settle actual contracted economics.
- **Equity Edge affiliate first-party page:** https://equityedge.io/affiliate advertises up to **30% on referred purchases**, creating a plausible independent incentive system for the separately observed Equity Edge ring. This does not establish that the three Hola Prime partner profiles were paid by Equity Edge or that their marketing contracts share an operator.

Taken together, these first-party terms prove that **legitimate paid conversion-focused affiliate schemes exist** and are capable of creating many independent promotional accounts without account factories. The three indexed bios and ref links establish **declared association and attribution links**; they do **not** establish actual purchase conversions, transfer of money, account administrator, or exclusive contractual restrictions.

## 6. Next discriminating evidence and competitive implications

**Priority 1 — original X records:** For `@sage_089`, `@Sunshine_godson`, `@phantom_1x`, and overlapping targets `@PropMonitorFX`, `@KrixxBanks`, `@Quantivor`, obtain X-authorized or voluntary export of original **status IDs, created_at UTC, in_reply_to_id, quote/repost target, author user ID**, and referral link. Construct event-level reply/quote/mention rates, proportional share of a uniform 30-day baseline, text-template similarity, mutual amplification and independent market-analysis share. Validate each account against its *own* normal behavior and a control group of nonpaid creators.

**Priority 2 — commercial/control differentiation:** Company/creator first-party acknowledgments, public partnership terms specific to each account, or optional explicitly supplied contracts. Account owners may be external affiliates, media operators, staff, independent investors, a common contractor, or a mixture. Co-occurrence and referral links alone cannot distinguish who holds the login or who drafts posts.

**Priority 3 — company evidence fidelity:** Compare sampled promoter financial/operational claims against the exact scoped upstream evidence. Classify independently-reviewed (defined population/window), company self-reported, outside scope, contradicted, and unresolved. In a product such as MastermindX, an original market thesis should remain traceable to time-appropriate source evidence rather than turning corporate PR into a repeated, apparently independent analyst endorsement.
