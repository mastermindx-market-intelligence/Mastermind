## Verification and limits

- **Directly evidenced:** specific referral-bearing posts, indexed official promotional terms, same-domain and cross-domain URL reuse in the prior case, and external regulators' descriptions of other proven schemes.
- **Not evidenced:** a shared ultimate legal owner of the original two major clusters; a sponsor contract for any named promotional beneficiary; control of the alleged bots by legitimate fintech firms; a direct promotional-payment link to @MichaelPBento.
- **Source caveats:** the legacy ledger contains third-party mirrors and historical snapshots. The current session did not independently authenticate the complete X post history. Moomoo source documents were search indexed but direct HTML retrieval was blocked.
- **Privacy:** This is a **public repository**. A prior direct WhatsApp contact link is redacted; do not add private messages, contact numbers, nonpublic identity information, account credentials or confidential Mastermind business data.
- **Reproducibility:** dates embedded inside campaign filenames are not publication timestamps. Exact domain spelling matters (e.g. premarketdesk.com versus premarketdesks.com). Duplicated page templates are not proof of identical ownership.
- **No accusations:** research labels describe observable patterns or hypotheses, not findings of criminal liability or confirmed impersonator identity.
