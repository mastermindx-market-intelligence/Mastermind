# Financial-X engagement research — creator economics and cross-brand network extension

**As of:** 2026-10-30 (North America)  
**Scope:** public primary-source program terms, one first-person founder interview, public creator profiles, and explicitly marked third-party X mirrors. Companion to [industry distribution comparison](industry_distribution_comparison.md), [interaccount networks](interaccount_networks_first_pass.md), and [typed graph](interaccount_edges_first_pass.json).  
**Status:** research-only; no verified attribution of covert company control or synthetic-user operator. Public repository: no personal contact details, nonpublic records, or Mastermind commercial secrets.

## Executive finding

The market shows several *disclosed* routes to multiply creator reach **without** a company owning creators' X accounts: fixed per-subscription referrals (TradingView), recurring affiliate revshare (LuxAlgo), performance-paid creators who may also hold company equity (Blossom), licensed influencer/model portfolios (dub Advisors), and rewarded public UGC contests (Moomoo; earlier record). An affiliate account may promote several competing firms while also cross-reposting and using recurring campaign templates. These links support commercial or editorial *affiliation*; they neither require bot accounts nor establish shared operator control.

The original StockSelectAI / SpinOfDesk / impersonation-related domain family remains unattributed. No evidence here identifies its owners or ties its conduct to the legitimate companies below.

**Business-model inference:** first-party paid-solicitation disclaimers (dub), last-click CPA attribution (Seeking Alpha), recurring revenue share (LuxAlgo), and performance-paid creator portfolios (Blossom) offer four clearly different mechanisms by which legitimate fintechs can leverage external creator accounts. Merely observing repeated brand mentions is inadequate to distinguish them.

### Comparability constraints

- TradingView >$15M vs LuxAlgo >$4.6M are **cumulative company-reported cross-platform commission totals** with nonidentical program lifespans and reporting cutoffs. **Neither is a validated X budget, number of X accounts, profit, or creator ROI.** Do not rank marketing productivity from aggregate absolute payouts alone.
- Blossom 40M+ **combined social follower counts** potentially include overlap and multiple platforms. No X-only active creator census, unique audience denominator, or paid X impression volume is given.
- A paid or equity-incentivized creator can be **independent**; corporate capital participation and referral status do not prove an employee login or editorial control.
- Even explicit disclosures of commercial incentives say nothing by themselves about deceptive participation, purchase fraud, duplicate platform accounts or undisclosed paid praise. Test these separately.

## 4. Implications for MastermindX distribution (strategy analysis only)

**Evidence → interpretation → potential test**, not an instruction to buy engagement or impersonate users:

1. **Audience ownership through useful product content:** TradingView and LuxAlgo turn chart/analysis content into affiliate-attributed subscriber conversions. Test a transparent creator marketplace and high-quality shareable source-grounded charts; count verified registrations and retained users, not raw reposts.
2. **Verified creator trust:** Blossom pays creators to direct an audience to **their own linked portfolios**. Test source-linked analytical watchlists/transparent thesis pages on Mastermind, with visible creator identity, terms, relevant disclosures and public accuracy/refresh history; do not suggest a creator owns positions without verified consent/data.
3. **Cross-brand creator recruitment:** Use the existing Equity Edge evidence as a discovery map of *independent* creators with portable communities, not a list of supposedly company-owned bots. Evaluate original research quality, audience fit and disclosure before any legitimate affiliate conversation.
4. **Avoid incentive-only comment loops:** giveaways can drive follows but confound organic engagement measurement. Any branded program should explicitly prohibit fake personas, undisclosed endorsements, artificial likes and coordinated manipulative stock recommendations.
5. **Measure funnel, not vanity network size:** creator-coded visits → engaged chart/analysis sessions → opt-in signup → seven-/30-day retention → paid conversions. Keep user-source consent, attribution windows, deduplication and compliance review separate.

These recommendations are inference and potential experiments; no product capability, acquisition cost, creator revenue, conversion or compliance review has been measured for Mastermind here.

## 5. Timestamp recovery from indexed post IDs — major mirror-recency correction

**New technical finding:** Some third-party X timeline mirrors expose actual **status URLs** in the clickable "View Details" controls, even though the mirror itself displays misleading relative dates such as "4 days ago" against an archive that is months old. The underlying 19-digit X status ID is time-encoded under Twitter's Snowflake convention. Its documented original implementation uses `twepoch = 1288834974657` and shifts the milliseconds by 22 bits. Original [Twitter Snowflake source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/twitter-archive/snowflake/snowflake-2010/src/main/scala/com/twitter/service/snowflake/IdWorker.scala) and [source repository](https://github.com/twitter-archive/snowflake/tree/snowflake-2010); the archived implementation is the origin of the formula, not proof every mirrored post is genuine.

For an intact authentic status ID, derive **UTC event time** using `new Date(Number((BigInt(id) >> 22n) + 1288834974657n)).toISOString()`. **Use BigInt**, never JavaScript floating-point conversion before shifting, or 19-digit IDs lose integer precision.

**Impact on this investigation:** a page labelled "4 days ago" cannot establish that content was posted during the current week; the above examples date to **June or August 2026**, outside the October 1–30 analysis window. This reduces false temporal edges, helps deduplicate mirrors by original status ID, and permits *bounded dated spot-checks* when links are available. **It still cannot measure post completeness, actual repost event times, private engagement, account ownership or 30-day frequency.**

**Updated collection procedure:** store `source_page_url`, `target_status_url`, `status_id`, `derived_created_at_utc`, `observed_author_handle`, `target/quoted_original_id`, `event_type`, `retrieved_at`, `confidence`, and `is_in_scope_window`. Treat mirror claims of retweets/replies as **mirror-observed** unless directly authenticated. Filter dated IDs first; never treat a snapshot's relative labels as today's recency. Do not change the prior 181-edge relationship graph based on a one-page sample.
