The co-occurrence of both personal accounts in unrelated creator-entry rules is consistent with a program designed to accumulate follows on the official brand, founder and affiliate coordinator simultaneously.

### Engagement architecture actually evidenced

(1) Corporate account + founder + affiliate coordinator act as **constant recipients**; (2) rotating creators host giveaways and add followers; (3) participants must repost quoted corporate and creator posts, and often follow additional promotional actors; (4) creators cross-repost, reply and occasionally announce purported winners; (5) several creator profiles provide tracked discounts or signup routes. Therefore, an explicit **growth mechanism** can explain a densely connected repost/comment network without bots, while allowing company-affiliated and genuinely independent accounts to coexist.

### Cross-brand replication

The same high-level entry method (follow brand and creator + like/repost + comment/tag) is visible around competitor prop-firm communities, e.g. @AlphaCapitalUK, @TradeifyFX, @FundedNext, @THEFUNDEDWAY. This is market-common incentive marketing, not exclusive fingerprint of Equity Edge. Specifically @De_CaptainChart and @skyshark0 participate across brands, and low-follower giveaway participants can amplify multiple unrelated offers. Campaign-source similarity should not be treated as a shared corporate administrator.

## Confidence and next proof

**High**: 1 brand and 2 self-declared leadership accounts recur as required follows in 22 distinct creators' indexed public giveaway posts; direct social promotion is observable. **High**: several creators advertise specific affiliate tokens and independent business identities. **Medium**: structured affiliate-program management explains recurrence; founder/affiliate roles are self-declared and partially corroborated but not corporate registry proof. **Low/unknown**: company owns creator accounts, controls all post text, pays for every giveaway, recovers conversion revenue per participant or deploys synthetic profiles.

Next: X-authorized 30-day post export for creator/leader set, stable user IDs and timestamps; measure creator-to-creator quote/reply frequency, reciprocation, hub share of mentions and follower growth; record disclosure and affiliate code per actor; seek publicly acknowledged partnership terms and distinct collaborations. Do not scrape private data or make bot/manipulation accusations based on these observations.

### Actual interaction edges versus participation instructions

The network contains at least three **different event classes**. Direct replies and reposts between creators, such as @AfaaqFx's response to @skyshark0's HolaPrime result and the cited @deartrader__ reposts of @Emrik__ / @theplaymakerxx, indicate social amplification. Contest **entry requirements** (follow three accounts, like, repost, comment) are calls-to-action, not proof every entrant performed the action. Affiliate **sign-up links** demonstrate campaign attribution infrastructure, not payout amounts or company ownership. Future quantitative network scoring must keep all three types distinct.
