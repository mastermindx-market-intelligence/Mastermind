- **Impersonation-themed campaign spread:** @Khynsy uses Bill Ackman-assistant branding and links three campaign domains; no verified authority from Bill Ackman (O49-O51).
- **Cross-channel endpoint linkage:** PremarketDesks /r/ redirect and independent @jima_nai public trading invitation use the exact same WhatsApp destination (O52-O53). Public post appears to predate the landing page URL date, but reply timestamp cannot be determined precisely.
- **Campaign-recipient corroboration:** @guibra_vo direct link resolves to WhatsApp label Marcus Edwards, matching @magaamerica1965 public display name (O54-O56). This is public persona matching, not legal identity verification.
- **Separate tracking links, imagery mismatch:** SpinOfDesk uses a /r/ redirect whose final destination cannot be recovered; photos used for one beneficiary differ across StockSelectAI and SpinOfDesk (O58-O59). Do not assume photos are real operator identities.
