## 1. Precedence and interpretation

Use **topic-specific precedence**, rather than making whichever document is newest globally authoritative:

1. Current Chairman intent and applicable protected source law govern the requested work. Read the compatible required law from one coherent protected pin; an open PR does not amend it.
2. Canonical owners govern their own facts: Executive for lifecycle/admission/effects, Agent OS for organizational continuity, GitHub for source/review/evidence, Capacity for eligible placement, and the incumbent release or installation owner for installed state. Linear is a selective projection; Slack is transport.
3. A narrower accepted owner amendment controls its topic. An exact later owner receipt can supersede an older factual next-action instruction without granting broader authority or erasing the earlier evidence.
4. Existing accepted plans retain their outcome and acceptance obligations until their actual owner changes them. Accepted source, installed bytes, enabled capability, successful execution, parent consumption and product acceptance remain separate.
5. Historical carrier names identify operations to recover. They do not prove an old session is live, require waiting for a retired chat, or permit replacement of its unresolved effects.
6. Candidate proposals remain useful design/review inputs. Their sequence is conditional on current custody, source acceptance, actual grants and production prerequisites.

These rules preserve the [convergence index](https://github.com/mastermindx-market-intelligence/Mastermind/blob/d010b161386853586198a43f9ea9f535bbebb377/docs/superpowers/plans/2026-08-27-mastermind-operating-surface-convergence-program-index.md), [convergence architecture](https://github.com/mastermindx-market-intelligence/Mastermind/blob/d010b161386853586198a43f9ea9f535bbebb377/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-27-mastermind-operating-surface-convergence-design.md) and [current ecosystem programme](https://github.com/mastermindx-market-intelligence/Mastermind/blob/d010b161386853586198a43f9ea9f535bbebb377/research/EXECUTIVE_ECOSYSTEM_COMPLETION_PROGRAMME_PLAN_2026-10-17.md).
