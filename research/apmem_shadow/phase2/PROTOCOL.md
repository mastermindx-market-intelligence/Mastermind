## Three independent scorecards

1. Forecast quality: paired common-row QLIKE and variance MSE, annual and predeclared era breakdowns, coverage/failure counts, forecast ratios, large underpredictions, cross-target and unretuned asset transfer. Evaluation eras: 2015-2019, 2020-2023, 2024-2026. All are retrospective and some market history has already been inspected; none is called untouched or prospective. Paired moving-block diagnostics use 63-session blocks, seed 20261113, 2,000 resamples; any pointwise/multiple-comparison distinction must be explicit.
2. Calibration and protection: rolling historical standardized-return lower quantiles (5% and 1%) versus Gaussian and unscaled historical controls; only prior matured residuals, 504-window/252-minimum. Report violations, interval uncertainty, clustering and quantile loss. Also report 90% upper variance envelopes based on trailing matured ratios. No distribution-free or tail-guarantee claim under dependent changing markets.
3. Economic utility and confluence: identical next-open long-only, unlevered baselines with and without the risk overlay. Baselines are fully invested, a 200-session trend exposure (1 above trend, .25 below), and a deliberately simple five-session contrarian exposure (1 after negative five-session return, .25 otherwise). Overlay min(1,target_vol/forecast_vol), targets 10% and 15%; primary 5-session forecast. Keep 5 and 20 bp turnover costs, extra-session delay and cash sensitivities. Compare to the same baseline, a training-era exposure-matched static reduction, and a clearly labeled hindsight exposure-matched diagnostic. Report CAGR, excess Sharpe, Sortino, realized vol, max drawdown, worst day, expected shortfall, ulcer index, upside/downside capture, turnover and exposure. Lower return alone is not rejection; lower drawdown caused merely by cash is not proof of timing skill. Do not combine all objectives into an optimized scalar trophy.
