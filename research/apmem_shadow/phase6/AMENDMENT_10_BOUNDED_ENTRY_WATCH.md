# Bounded entry-watch experiment: event, repair state and invalidation are distinct

Outcome-guided extension after inspecting the supplied MCD case, recovered phase6 results and inference-resolution audit. This is not a holdout, a live order queue, a new lifecycle owner or a universal bottom rule. Preserve original signal/source histories and all negative studies. No thresholds are selected using new outcomes.

## Hypothesis and fixed construction
The native dot is an observation, not necessarily immediate entry permission. A pure research state machine may retain that observation while waiting for already-defined closed slow/cohort support, without requiring every factor to improve on the exact trigger day. Unlike the rejected phase5 market-only waiting rule, this candidate conditions on the security's slow state and its leave-one-out economic cohort. Test rather than assume this distinction creates value.

For each native settled grey-dot observation, remember its known session and contemporaneous 21-session closing low. While no simulated position has yet been entered, retain at most one pending observation per instrument/family for up to21 trading sessions. A close below that fixed reference low cancels the pending observation before any same-day readiness check. Missing own/slow/cohort context cancels or abstains rather than being benign. A newly observed dot during a pending episode cannot rewrite its timestamp or reference; record it as a co-observation. After cancellation or expiry, only a new dot can create a new pending observation. The pending watch is not an invested position and this cancellation is not a post-entry stop.

Compare exactly four entry policies: immediate native dot; dot accepted only when slow/cohort support exists on the same day; finite watch until both support states exist; fixed-five-session wait with the same pre-entry low-break cancellation. The states reuse support_state.py unchanged. Do not require positive trailing relative level or a fully positive monthly oscillator. Actual readiness is observed on its later day and simulated entry occurs at the next close. No backdating, post-entry stop, leverage or optimized profit target.
