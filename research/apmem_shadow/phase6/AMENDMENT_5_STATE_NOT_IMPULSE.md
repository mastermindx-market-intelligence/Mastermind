# Amendment 5: state is not an impulse

## Evidence already inspected
The original 3,420 account evaluations and chronological classifier scores have completed. Aggregate classifier loss did not establish incremental directional value from the new cohort block; none of its 48 corrected comparisons passed. Initial cohort account results showed severe participation loss under simultaneous slow-improvement AND same-day peer-repair conditions. The MCD behavioral cohort chosen in September (YUM/CL/KO) had a positive 21-session return by October 20, but the peer-repair flag was false. These observations motivated this extension; it is explicitly outcome-guided and the original negative results remain.

## Semantic distinction
A positive change in breadth is a repair impulse. Once breadth becomes broad and remains high, its change can be zero. Calling that absence of repair evidence 'a bad cohort' confuses a derivative with a state. The converse also holds: breadth can improve slightly while still being broadly impaired. This is a mechanism/representation problem, not a reason to tune an arbitrary threshold until a backtest wins.

## Finite fixed alternatives
1. `cohort_healthy`: valid coverage, at least one half of peers above their 20-session averages, and positive 21-session peer total return. This can be true without new acceleration.
2. `cohort_supported`: healthy OR a prior observed repair impulse within 21 sessions that has not subsequently been invalidated by negative five-session peer return AND below-half participation AND increasing new-low pressure. Unknown current coverage makes the current state unknown/false-for-selection and clears the retained research support. No permanent bullish latch.
3. Phase-integrated slow support: average the two predeclared completed-calendar 2W histograms and average their last changes, requiring both inputs known. Support is positive mean histogram OR improving mean histogram. No choice of a favorable calendar pairing; correlated estimates are not independent votes. Preserve the raw values and their different known clocks.

Test on the unchanged native G0 and RSI-MACD-cross event tapes: raw; original impulse conjunction; slow support alone; cohort-supported alone; slow support plus cohort-supported; and that combination with positive short relative acceleration. Use the original same-name 21/63-session contracts and 5/20bp costs. Keep economic and previously selected behavioral cohort identities distinct. The positive-relative-level rule is not silently replaced.

This extension changes scientific feature semantics, not a production entry gate. Preserve all results, compare participation/exposure and return/risk/loss objectives separately, and qualify any apparent improvement against the raw and original filtered controls. No retrospective 'second bottom' label or automatic probability/position authority.
