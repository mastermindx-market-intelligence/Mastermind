# Pre-evaluation method clarification v1

This clarification is recorded before the first actual-market-data fit or evaluation of this original component. The original screenshot/audit and selected price/schema rows are already known; nothing here makes the historical strategy-selection sample untouched.

1. The QML objective discards the first 20 training observations as an initialization burn-in. Initialization and scale are estimated from that training window only. The fit remains a retrospective fit performed now, with a historical logical cutoff, not a fit proven to have existed at that historical time.
2. Add an analytically motivated forecast control: EWMA(0.94) of the unadjusted daily range, multiplied by the overnight-adjustment factor for the next session. That factor is already known at the forecast origin under the documented lagged recursion. This avoids giving APMEM an artificially weak comparison which ignores an available input. Also retain the last-proxy baseline. Report all controls on identical valid target rows, not the best selected control alone.
3. In addition to total price return, exposure and drawdown, report realized open-mark volatility and a zero-cash-rate Sharpe diagnostic. Low exposure and lower drawdown alone do not establish alpha. Sample uncertainty and overlapping events remain explicit. Count event origins coinciding with parameter refits; a new coefficient set can itself change forecast level.
4. Seek a longer approved Yahoo/yfinance raw-OHLC history, with corporate-action fields retained, to extend the existing 2017-onward reconstructed options archive through 2018, 2020 and 2022 stress regimes. Massive's current-vintage daily panel remains a separate cross-source check. Do not relabel adjusted opens as actual executable raw prices. The v1 portfolio replay remains the declared raw-price/no-distribution replay; any distribution accounting is separately labeled.
5. Use QQQ as the primary study. SPY and IWM are fixed, unretuned asset-transfer diagnostics only when their existing options archives and lawful price inputs are available. Their common equity-market exposure means they are not independent replications. Do not substitute another symbol after seeing an unfavorable result. No sector-transfer claim is authorized.
6. The source-clock consumer must retain unknown bar/publication clocks as unknown; it may prove present capture, but cannot manufacture a historical observed-through or first-availability timestamp from a date-only parquet index. A real invocation which abstains is proof of the rejection path, not a working production Prophet integration.
