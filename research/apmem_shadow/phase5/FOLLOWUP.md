# Independent recovery falsifiers and bounded exploratory follow-up

The original 242 accounts and 48 paired-return contrasts have been executed on nine sector ETFs. No return contrast passed the full Holm correction. The primary phase0 pre-2020 21-session grey-dot account had CAGR3.5502%, Sharpe.4227 and max drawdown-25.8965%; the same-day market-repair subset had CAGR3.7163%, Sharpe.6734 and drawdown-13.3172%, at lower exposure. The six-session memory/repair sequence was not robust in older crises. The small post-reset sample was substantially more favorable to stress/recovery and unfavorable to rising-volatility vetoes. These are exploratory observations, not promotion or untouched validation.

## New questions after seeing the results: explicitly exploratory

The apparent same-day repair signal is worth challenging, not crowning. Freeze the following small set before running it, without parameter search:
- Slow-supported repair: native dot + market repair + sector close above its126-session mean. This tests the orderly-pullback mechanism, not exact-bottom calling.
- Declining low pressure: native dot + market repair + across-sector20-session new-low frequency over the latest5 sessions below the prior5-session frequency. It tests whether pressure is easing; it is not another copy of a deep oversold threshold.
- Prior-leader rotation: native dot + market repair + recipient positive5-session total return while the equal-weight prior two63-session leaders have negative5-session return. Leaders are selected from information21 sessions earlier, never chosen to make the present chart look good.
- Ongoing repair policy: retain the original dot+repair entries but permit a next-close exit after two consecutive sessions of negative equal-sector5-session return and deteriorating above20-day participation. There is no percentage stop or target. This tests continuing cohort support versus an entry-only snapshot. Horizon caps21/63 and costs5/20bp remain.
