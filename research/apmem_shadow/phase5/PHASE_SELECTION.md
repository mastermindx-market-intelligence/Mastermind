# Phase-conditioned sector selection - bounded exploratory experiment

Already observed: repaired dot outcomes largely track the same-date sector-market payoff; the unconditional top3 leader controls fail in the later sample; the six-session dot-memory hypothesis is not robust historically. This motivates one distinct question: does sector selection need a different relative-momentum sign during recovery than during continuation? This is explicitly outcome-guided, not an untouched trial. It does not erase prior nulls or confer authority.

Same nine ETFs, source/vintage limitations, reserved interval, phase0 native records, evaluation windows, next-close timing and5/20bp costs. No new provider, model or threshold search.

Market recovery event: recent widespread stress AND positive equal-sector5-session return AND increasing above20-day participation AND declining across-sector20-day-new-low frequency AND at least one settled native grey dot on the current date. Continuation event: no recent widespread stress, positive equal-sector5-session return, at least five of nine sectors above their126-session mean AND at least one registered sector continuation breakout. Event requires absence of that market condition for three sessions. A market event is one episode, not nine independent sector discoveries.

At each common event date compare: all nine sectors; top3 by total return from63 sessions earlier to5 sessions earlier; bottom3 by the same score. Freeze the exclusion of the latest five sessions so the ranking measures prior relative damage/leadership rather than selecting the immediate bounce. All securities must have qualified inputs. The selected sectors need not each carry a grey dot: the experiment tests timing versus selection. Keep a tag that this differs from a per-sector dot entry.

Two position constructions: equal weights summing to1, and the same slot weights multiplied by min(1,15%/annualized EWMA .94 squared-return volatility). Unused risk budget stays cash; no leverage or renormalization. Sample first21-session outcomes for sector-relative event payoff, but count events/dates not securities as independent. Record differences between winners, losers and all-sector returns, adverse path, and capital exposure.

For executable counterfactuals use a single unlevered portfolio: open only when flat, fill at next close, keep shares/total-return units fixed until21/63-session close exit, ignore overlapping new events while held. Fees are charged on actual buys/sells, no use of intraday future proceeds, and no stops or optimized exits. Compare recovery and continuation schedules separately. Then test one fixed router: choose bottom3 in recovery and top3 in continuation, with equal-sector and always-top3 routers as controls. Preserve every cell, both eras and all fees, not only the best row.

Interpretation can be: (a) common timing but no sector-selection edge, (b) phase-specific selection candidate, or (c) no useful advantage. No universal winner is forced. If a row appears attractive, disclose selected-history dependence, event count, costs and whether risk differences explain it. This finishes a bounded mechanism test, not an open-ended parameter search.
