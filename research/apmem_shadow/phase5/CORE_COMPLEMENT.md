# Sector carry versus recovery complement - disclosed exploratory extension

The original dot/recovery experiment has been observed. Its sparse event sleeves spend much of their time in cash. A faster entry marker cannot solve the separate question of maintaining participation in persistent sector leadership. This extension tests that distinction; it is not a newly untouched trial and does not change the original protocols/results.

Use the SAME nine ETFs, private source snapshots, excluded2020-May2023 interval, post-gap warmup, decision clocks and fee assumptions. Never read the local team's held-back outcomes. Four fixed monthly controls, no parameter search:

1. Top3 sectors by total-return momentum from126 sessions ago to21 sessions ago, one-third target per selected sector, including negative-momentum leaders as a deliberately simple long-only control.
2. Same ranking but require the sector price above its126-session mean and positive126-to21-session momentum. Vacant slots remain cash; do not renormalize fewer names to full exposure.
3. Same as2 with each slot multiplied by min(1,15%/annualized EWMA(.94) daily-return volatility). No leverage. This uses the simple risk baseline; it is not mislabeled the original OHLC-target APMEM model.
4. All nine sectors satisfying the SAME trend conditions, one-ninth target each. This tests selection versus a common timing effect.

Targets are observed at the last actual trading close of each month and filled at the next trading CLOSE, with a separately declared first-evaluation-date initialization. Solve transaction-cost-funded post-trade equity, keep cash nonnegative, and mark drifted positions between rebalances. Primary5bp and20bp-per-side sensitivity; cash yield0%. Total-return-index simulation is not a broker ledger or a raw-share corporate-action settlement proof.

For each control, compare fixed initial50/50 core plus raw-dot, core plus dot-and-market-repair, and core plus cash. Tactical sleeves use the already frozen21/63-session phase0 outcomes, not a hindsight best marker/horizon. No mixture weights are tuned. Report full return/risk/exposure tables, not just the largest CAGR; the portfolios are distinct risk/participation choices, not equivalent-risk claims.

Run 2007-2019 and the independently reset post-gap segment separately. Retain all results and input/code hashes. These are high-dependence exploratory tests on previously inspected historical data. No capital authority, live deployment, automatic schedule or new service follows. The principal remains the implementation/research owner.
