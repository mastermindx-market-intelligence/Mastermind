This implements the ETF lane already declared in PROTOCOL.md. The stock primary results have now been inspected, so ETF-specific comparisons are disclosed as transfer/exploratory evidence, not a pristine independent confirmation. No new forecast model or threshold is fitted.

Reuse the eleven accepted phase2 ETF panels and saved `moment_repair/*_predictions.npz` outputs. Bind each forecast array to the exact prepared-price SHA256 in the original phase2 numerical receipt and the identical ordered date index. Reconstruct phase3 total-return signal inputs and verify common-target variance and close-return equality against those original prepared observations. Record current output-file hashes; do not silently regenerate or optimize phase2 forecasts. The first raw row lacks an overnight return and is not assigned a fabricated forecast.

Five sector panels use the already qualified constant-share-unit split adapter. Their transformed `stock_splits` column is zero and original action metadata remains separately retained; never apply the original split again. Raw/qualified panel and numerical input hashes are carried to the transfer receipt. Volume/liquidity conventions remain source-derived; these highly liquid ETFs are not evidence that the same fills are available in small-cap stocks.

Replay the unchanged native no-peer-waiver gate through the same January 2015-to-June 2, 2026 signal interval as the stock account. Use the same 2020-01-02-to-2026-06-02 portfolio window; all 21/63-session outcomes have matured inside the preserved November12 data endpoint. This avoids filtering account opportunities merely because the final observations are label-censored. The fixed ETF panel is its own declared universe, not fictitious SP1500 membership. XLF remains excluded for its unqualified in-kind action basis.

Fixed transfer accounts: union barrier unscaled, EWMA-sized, MEM-sized; union time21; union stop21; union trail63 unscaled and MEM-sized; T1 and T2 individually; breakout20, mean50 reclaim and positive63/126 onset controls. Keep the same eight-percent new-position allocation, ten-slot and eighty-percent decision-open gross limits; ETF forecast-volatility target is fifteen percent as preregistered. No price-only stock classifier or learned twenty-one-day probability is transferred to ETFs or the sixty-three-day exit contract.

Report all accounts, event coverage and event-conditioned forecast losses. Eleven correlated ETFs are not eleven independent trials; overlapping sector/index exposures are not a fully diversified portfolio. This is a test of whether component behavior transfers across instruments, not certified production portfolio construction or an options strategy.
