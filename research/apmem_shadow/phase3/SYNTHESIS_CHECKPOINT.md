## Central informational finding

On the same970 chronological union-entry predictions, frequency/price/risk/interaction total multiclass log losses are 1.06898964 / .96937820 / .95629413 / .95754809. The exact likelihood decomposition is more informative than the headline improvement:

| Model | Activity/timeout log loss | Direction log loss conditional on a barrier being hit | Active events |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Rolling frequency | .53722901 | .67250040 | 767 |
| Price/tier features | .42651720 | .68653867 | 767 |
| Added forecast-risk features | .41360477 | .68632162 | 767 |
| Added interactions | .41551200 | .68549545 | 767 |

All improvement over the frequency baseline comes from distinguishing movement from timeout; the directional component offsets some of that improvement. This does not establish that no directional edge is possible. It establishes that THIS probability model's lower total loss must not be marketed as learning which direction pays. The additional MEM-risk improvement over price features does not survive the original paired statistical tests. The model's job should be disclosed as activity/path-risk research, not autonomous opportunity selection.
