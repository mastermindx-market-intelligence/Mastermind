# Accounting qualification and finite comparisons - pre-outcome addendum

Recorded before phase3 market-outcome inspection. Source mechanics and acquisition coverage have been inspected; no model/strategy outcome has been compared. Preserve the original protocol and all excluded panel members.

## Corporate actions and provider boundary

Official Tiingo EOD documentation defines divCash at the ex-date, while detailed distributions distinguish paymentDate. It also describes splitFactor as a price-adjustment factor for splits/reverse splits/distributions. Therefore an ex-date entitlement is not immediately spendable money, and an arbitrary factor cannot be asserted to create parent-company shares.

One bounded read of the existing detailed split endpoint returned HTTP 403. That action was stopped, with the original receipt retained; no retry, account switch, alternative protected endpoint or credential change follows. The independent EOD captures remain valid observed inputs. No claim of detailed split/payment-date qualification is made.

Primary cash accounting holds all cash-distribution entitlements as non-interest-bearing, unspendable receivables through the evaluation end. They are marked in wealth but cannot finance another purchase. This is a deliberately conservative liquidity convention, NOT the actual payment schedule. A separately labelled ex-date-spendable sensitivity is the opposite liquidity approximation. Neither fabricates a payment date. Portfolio and individual-trade total wealth include earned receivables; receivable timing and spendable cash remain distinct.

An EOD factor close (relative tolerance 1e-5) to an integer 1..20, its reciprocal, or 1.5 is retained only as a declared ordinary-ratio research unit transformation, not independent issuer confirmation. Nonmatching factors quarantine the entire selected instrument without replacement. This rule additionally quarantines REZI (1.437) and CTVA (6.665), before outcomes; WOR and EBAY were already quarantined by unexplained adjustment-factor changes. Rounding in source ratios is retained, not silently rewritten. A no-split-bearing-instrument sensitivity removes every instrument with any nonunit factor and is required before interpreting aggregate economic results. The stronger limitation remains: these are reconstructed source-based research returns, not a certified broker replay.

Source references: https://www.tiingo.com/documentation/end-of-day ; https://www.tiingo.com/documentation/corporate-actions/splits ; https://www.tiingo.com/documentation/corporate-actions/dividends . These describe source semantics, not our data entitlements or empirical performance.

## Portfolio window and exposure

Primary common account window is 2020-01-02 through 2026-06-02, with no new admissions beyond the frozen membership snapshot. Existing positions are liquidated at the common last close and boundary-trade counts are reported. Event outcomes may mature later for full-horizon path analysis, but no post-cutoff signal enters the account. Opening exposure after orders is recorded separately from closing exposure, so a same-day stop does not appear to have used no risk capital.

The relative-strength comparison retains positive filtering and descending rank as distinct arms. Negative/unknown classifier expected payoff is not replaced with positive information. Classifier fits refresh on the first eligible event origin at least 63 calendar trading sessions after the preceding attempt. Failed/insufficient refreshes abstain; no stale fitted bundle is silently carried forward. Learning uses a maximum trailing 1260-session training-origin window, all full outcome endpoints matured, and ridge 1.0 on total negative log likelihood.
