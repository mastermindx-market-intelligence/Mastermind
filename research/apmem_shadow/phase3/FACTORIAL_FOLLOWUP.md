# Outcome-guided factorial follow-up - explicitly exploratory

The original 95-account run and all34 primary tests are complete and retained. This follow-up is motivated by those inspected outcomes; it is NOT part of the original preregistration and cannot inherit untouched-holdout or confirmatory language. No indicator thresholds, model fits or input histories are retuned.

## Why this follow-up is necessary

The original union entry with a fixed 5% stop/8% target/21-session horizon returned about 3.83% annualized, while the same entry family with the registered uncapped 63-session trailing exit returned about 12.36%, with appreciably worse drawdown and adverse-tail losses. These are point estimates, not a statistically established return improvement after the original family correction. MEM sizing reduced adverse-tail loss, but its incremental event-conditioned forecasting/probability gains were not established relative to the stronger controls.

A breakout or persistent-trend entry may be poorly served by a short reversal-style profit cap. Therefore evaluating all momentum representations only under that one exit would not answer the Chairman's functional-component question. The next test crosses component jobs rather than adding indicators.

## Fixed complete factorial, with reuse

Six existing entry families (T1, T2, union, breakout20, mean50 reclaim, positive63/126 onset) x four existing exit contracts (time21, stop21, barrier21, trail63) x three existing entry-size policies (none, EWMA, MEM) = 72 fixed cells. All other primary accounting, membership, liquidity, costs, capital caps and data limitations are unchanged. Reuse byte-bound original accounts for matching cells; execute only the previously uncomputed cells. Print the entire matrix, not just the best row. These cells overlap heavily and are not 72 independent discoveries.

Report 2020-2023 and 2024-2026 portions without selecting a preferred subperiod. The earlier design history and this follow-up's selection remain disclosed. A positive row is a candidate for new forward qualification, not automatic promotion. No new classifier is trained for the 63-session contract.

## Attribution and robustness rather than a backtest trophy

For the original union barrier and uncapped-trailing arms, add transparent per-lot spent/received/contribution fields without changing any account action. Verify their daily equity paths remain equal to the previously retained runs. Reconcile the sum of closed-lot cash/receivable P&L to final account wealth under zero cash interest. Report contributions by name, largest winners, gap losses, repeated stops and terminal-liquidation effects.

Compare the trailing arm with a constant-size version matched to the original barrier arm's mean decision-open exposure. That match uses hindsight and is strictly an accounting diagnostic, not a deployable rule. Report the same-entry per-path payoff difference before capital competition as a separate quantity. Remove the top contributing name and the top three contributing names in two labelled, outcome-selected fragility checks; these removals are not new investable universes or model-selection rules.

Compare a fixed 50/50 persistent-opportunity/tactical mixture with an equally fixed 50/50 persistent-opportunity/cash control. No mixing weight is optimized. This distinguishes a useful tactical sleeve from the simple benefit of holding more cash. Portfolio covariance qualification and production sizing authority remain separate obligations.

No source result is erased when it disappoints. This follow-up tests whether payoff horizon, contribution concentration and capital occupancy explain the apparent return improvement more directly than additional technical confluence.
