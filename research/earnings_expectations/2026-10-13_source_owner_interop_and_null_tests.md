## Selected-case cloud beat versus next-session stock response

Among the seven contemporaneous media-reported cloud-growth consensus witnesses already in `media_reported_expectations.json`, correlate reported actual-minus-expected **year-over-year cloud growth percentage-point** surprise with the corresponding issuer's retrospective adjusted next-session percent price return.

| Check | Result | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| Sample | 7 selected events | Three AI-cloud megacaps, not a representative universe |
| Pearson r | +0.02409 | Virtually no linear relationship **within these selected observations** |
| Exact two-sided permutation tail proportion | 0.95218 | 5,040 possible return-label permutations; exploratory, not a population-level causal test |
| Leave-one-event-out r range | −0.2308 to +0.5897 | Extreme sensitivity to a single event |

All seven expectation figures were quoted by articles **after** earnings and lack admitted analyst contributor/vintage/rights, so this calculation is retrospective descriptive research, never a historical model feature. Cross-company Azure/AWS/Google Cloud segment growth is economically heterogeneous. There are no pre-registered holdout, proper factor controls, risk-neutral option expectations, firm forward-guide surprise or realistic trading cost model. Do **not** interpret the above p-value as proving absence of an expectation-repricing relationship. It only shows that a naive, one-dimensional cloud beat/rally score is not justified by this tiny casebook.
