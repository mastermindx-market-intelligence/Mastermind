# 02 — Integrated product masterplan

This overlay organizes delivery under the existing #1202 mission. It does not replace the domain plans, modify protected law, or grant runtime/source/data rights. Use [01_CENSUS](01_CENSUS.md) for current state and [03_WORK_PACKAGES](03_WORK_PACKAGES.md) for bounded assignments.

## 1. North star and concrete user workflow

A user opens a company or theme in Mastermind and can answer: **What changed? What did the available evidence previously imply? Which financial/operating consequences are supportable? Which related securities are actually showing strength? What is uncertain, stale, contradicted or not yet measurable?** The user can inspect the source, timing, calculation and provenance of each answer and revisit the same cutoff after later corrections.

The product should join evidence and interpretation without pretending that a persuasive explanation is a validated forecast. Price/tape context may prioritize investigation; it must not circularly prove the thesis that produced the candidate. Institutional holdings, short-volume measurements and price residuals are not interchangeable evidence of current fund inflow.

### Three deliverable surfaces

| Surface | Required initial behavior | Explicit exclusions |
|---|---|---|
| Ticker/news change view | Correct, timely, entitled, non-repetitive story feed with correction/withdrawal behavior, health and source detail inside Terminal | No fake AI sentiment, unsupported impact score, unlicensed body redistribution or dependence on completed GMI |
| Expectations/evidence dossier | Cutoff-aware native capture inspection; qualified actuals/guidance and comparable expectation deltas where supported; scenarios and unknowns shown separately | No guessed identity/basis, universal gap score, implied market consensus from price alone or uncalibrated options probability |
| Theme/leadership context | Canonical PIT memberships, existing residual/rotation evidence, provenance and descriptive leader/laggard context | No second graph/ranker, inferred natural history, unsupported holdability profile or reopening of closed studies |

The eventual integrated answer connects those surfaces through existing owner references. It should show event facts, source overlap, economic mechanism, deterministic conditional financial consequences, compatible expectations when available, separate tape/positioning context, falsifiers and next observations. It should refuse an unsupported join rather than fill the gap with model prose.

## 2. Definition of completion

Report completion on separate dimensions:

**Source:** producer and reader contracts, revisions, rights, native clocks, identity and coverage are qualified at exact revisions. **Product:** an entitled user completes the named workflow through the actual deployed consumer, including degraded/refused states. **Operations:** a natural permitted event/report crosses the real path with measured latency, recovery and correction behavior. **Science:** each admitted hypothesis receives its registered verdict, including null/rejection; an underpowered study remains accruing, not complete. **Promotion:** any stronger use is explicitly accepted by the existing evaluator and consuming authority.

A descriptive product can finish while a distinct forward study remains held. The whole program cannot be labeled done while a contracted product capability is missing, a live consumer has not been proved, or an open study is represented as resolved. A valid null can close the study and remove its proposed feature rather than create perpetual research work.

### Minimum golden-vertical acceptance

The existing C19 H07/MAS-274 golden vertical remains behind its recorded MAS-268/271/272/273 dependencies. This overlay does not silently relax that gate. It requires one genuinely permitted event-to-driver answer from real owner artifacts, with exact evidence generations and a real nonbinding reader. C19's synthetic gauntlet and health work remain separate prerequisites for later empirical promotion/scale where applicable.

Before that full vertical is ready, the news view and properly restricted native capture inspection can deliver independent value through their own accepted contracts. Calling them useful intermediate products must not rename them H07 acceptance or a calibrated signal.

## 6. Horizontal delegation without turning frontier models into coders

One principal owns the cross-product dependency/acceptance decisions. Use Astra or another eligible Sol-class local principal under the current route policy; Fable is justified for unresolved cross-owner semantics and sustained integration, not every task. Domain incumbents retain their scoped ownership until an actual transfer is settled. Do not create two equal CEOs issuing writes to the same domain.

Builders receive small, independently testable packets. Fast approved workers handle contract fixtures, exact mapping checks, straightforward adapters, component behavior and ordinary fixes; standard engineering workers handle multi-file source/API integration; frontier judgment is reserved for genuine conflicting contracts, novel effect recovery and acceptance. Names such as GLM, Qwen or MiniMax are candidate configured worker avenues only if currently admitted and suitable; this document asserts no provider limits, model quality, quota or credential availability.

Use the existing Capacity/Model Router's live admissible slots and remaining provider limits. The concurrency ceiling is the minimum of ready path-disjoint work, available runtime leases, host capacity, approved provider concurrency and review/return capacity. This is scheduling guidance for the incumbent scheduler, not a new scheduler implementation. Do not fix a universal '20 workers' count or bypass a provider cap with alternate accounts.

Keep enough review capacity to consume returns rather than accumulating unreviewed branches. Shared source files and CI enrollment have one writer/integrator. Test authors, read-only reviewers and builders can work concurrently only when their inputs and mutation envelopes are explicit. Small independent PRs plus deterministic integration tests are safer throughput than a many-writer megabranch.
