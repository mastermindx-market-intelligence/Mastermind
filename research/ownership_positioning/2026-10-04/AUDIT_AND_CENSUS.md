**Commercial rights:** only public vendor documentation was reviewed. A lawful sample, negotiated delivery, historical correction replay and rights to retain/display/derive are needed before source admission or purchase. No approximate dollar quotes are manufactured.

**Scientific validity:** the report designs tests but does not establish alpha, causal intent or a risk-forecast uplift. Source overlap and statistical dependence remain different problems. A null predictive result can coexist with useful descriptive context.

**Review:** this is a source-grounded author audit with mechanical publication checks, not a second independent reviewer, legal counsel, production security audit or empirical replication. The PR's review status must not be described as accepted until that review actually occurs.
