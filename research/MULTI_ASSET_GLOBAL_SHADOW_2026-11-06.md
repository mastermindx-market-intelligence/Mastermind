## Product bridge

`portfolio/global_shadow_research.py` lets a caller compare **explicitly supplied, hypothetical** fully funded portfolios across the fixed eleven US-listed ETF proxies already monitored by Macro's daily Risk Radar: SPY, EFA, EEM, FXI, KWEB, GLD, HYG, SHY, TLT, USO and IBIT. Cash is a separately specified economic destination; SHY is *not cash*. This module does not broaden actual AI book eligibility (native Mastermind `portfolio/instrument_policy.py` remains authoritative) or use any customer holdings.

The adapter qualifies descriptive source metadata and labels, original price/receipt limitations, exact instrument family, listing, currency, interval, benchmark, relative wealth arithmetic, source status, identities, corrections and missingness **before** constructing a research-only universe. It excludes unobserved instruments entirely. Foreign-region assets are represented only by **US-listed USD price proxies**, not actual China/Hong Kong venue prices or hedged local-currency returns. Parent `portfolio/global_scenario_context.compare` remains the **sole** calculation owner for fully funded weights, returns, costs charged once, exposure limits, cash, and hypothetical scenario status. No new source reader, scheduler, warehouse, score, book, queue, policy or order path is introduced.

The returned `market_context` holds `status`, last session, expected session, digest, coverage, eligible/excluded symbols and `source_authenticated=false`. The input hash **shape** is checked, but source cryptographic/transport authenticity cannot be established from a caller's JSON alone. The top-level `status` means **mathematical calculation qualification** only; it does not upgrade market-context freshness. `scenario_basis=hypothetical_not_forecast`, `no_predictive_claim=true` and every capital authority flag is false. No ranking, recommendation, crash probability, historical alpha, target weights or intraday delivery claim is made.

The only permitted book identity here is `global_research_shadow`, `research_only=true`, `active_account=false`, explicit USD basis, synthetic revision, snapshot validity, fully funded positions/cash, and caller-declared limits. Active account IDs, tenant IDs, supplied instrument-universe permissions, broker IDs and auth material are refused, not normalized into research authority. A caller may compare hold, cash, partial rotation and staged reentry assumptions but may never submit them for execution through this module. All scenario returns are independently declared **hypotheses**, never sourced from historical observed returns.
