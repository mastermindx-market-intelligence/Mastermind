## 4. Findings

**F1 — MINOR — `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-pbr-experiment-registry-and-protocols.md:319`**
The ladder's fourth rung truncates a misuse control. The spec renders it "Diagnostic only; not causal alpha",
while `MASTERPLAN.md:281` reads "Diagnostic only **when based on realized exposure**; do not call it causal alpha."
Dropping the realized-exposure condition would let a later lane build the lagged exposure-matched comparison from
*intended* or *projected* exposure — the exact misuse the masterplan guards against. The per-row citation is
correct, so a lane that follows it recovers the full control; hence MINOR, not MAJOR.
*Smallest fix:* restore the condition in that cell → "Diagnostic only when based on realized exposure; not causal alpha".

**F2 — MINOR — `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-pbr-experiment-registry-and-protocols.md:164`**
"corrections classified separately" is cited to `[MP §4 A02–A03, lines 199–200]`. Those two register rows identify
the *affected records* (A02: NAV falls back to average cost for a missing price; A03: historical mark selection can
use an undated Polygon snapshot), not the classification *rule*. The claim itself is true and is already supported
by the E0 row the spec cites one bullet earlier (`MASTERPLAN.md:469`, "labeled correction analyses"); the direct
statements of the rule are `MASTERPLAN.md:354` ("Preserve the original ledger and corrections rather than
overwriting inconvenient history"), `:362` ("Corrections create new linked records rather than rewriting what a
previous manager could know") and `:445` ("A correction cannot masquerade as contemporaneous source evidence").
No floor and no marker is involved, so this does not reach the A9 MAJOR bar.
*Smallest fix:* change the citation to `[MP §7.4, line 362; MP §8, line 445]`, optionally keeping A02–A03 as
"(affected records: A02–A03)".

**F3 — MINOR — `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-pbr-experiment-registry-and-protocols.md:179`**
Predicate misattribution. The spec says "Volatility-scaled and correlation/factor-aware anchors are candidates, not
automatic winners [MP §7.6, line 389]", but `MASTERPLAN.md:389` applies that exact predicate to **hierarchical
methods** ("Hierarchical methods are candidates, not automatic winners"); volatility-scaled and factor-aware anchors
appear there as members of the compare set. The spec's version is neutral-to-stricter and changes no arm, but as
written it silently drops the masterplan's hierarchical-methods caveat.
*Smallest fix:* "Volatility-scaled, correlation/factor-aware, and hierarchical anchors are candidates, not
automatic winners [MP §7.6, line 389]."
