## Findings

**F1 — MINOR — `scripts/remove_positions.py:139-144`, `scripts/scrub_cn_tiny_trims.py:107-110`.** The duplicate-revise skip keys on `row_identity` alone (`if identity in revised_identities: continue`). For any writer-produced ledger this is exactly right: identical identity ⟹ byte-equal content ⟹ same `asof`, and the first revise's pointer supersedes every same-identity copy. But a hand-edited ledger carrying the same well-formed stored `row_id` on two rows with **different** `asof` shares an identity *without* the second being superseded (cross-`asof` pointers are ignored, `bot/decision_rows.py:236-237`); the second row's scrub is then skipped while its drops are still counted, whereas 5c4a8658 would have revised and scrubbed it. Unreachable through the writers (`row_id` is content-derived at `bot/decision_rows.py:176`), so this is corruption-edge polish, not a live defect. Smallest fix: key `revised_identities` on `(identity, asof)` in both scripts.

**F2 — MINOR — `tests/test_decision_rows_supersedes.py:148-173`, `bot/decision_rows.py:244-247`.** The new long-chain test is non-discriminating by construction: it passes unchanged on 5c4a8658 (run and verified), and the pure-chain shape it builds exhibits no old-vs-new timing delta (measured 0.9–1.2× at n=300/1200) because only the chain *head* survives and walks — the old code was already linear there. The memoization is real where the degenerate case actually lives — many surviving revise rows sharing one chain — measured 3.6× at k=m=200 growing to 6.4× at k=m=400 with identical output (quadratic → linear). The new comment "a long revise chain used to be re-walked for every surviving row" overstates: only revise-mode survivors with a revise target walk at all. B2's fail-on-old demand is unsatisfiable for a semantics-preserving memoization (F8's own mandate said "identical semantics"), so this is test/comment polish, not a defect. Smallest fix: reword the comment ("for every surviving *revise* row that joins the chain"), and optionally add a call-count or wall-clock assertion on the shared-chain shape, which is the shape that actually regresses.
