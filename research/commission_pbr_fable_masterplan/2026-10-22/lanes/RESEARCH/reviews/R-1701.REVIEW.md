## Findings

F1. MINOR — `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-pbr-pm-facts-thesis-risk-budget.md:63`. Rule (i) does not state how a primary-evidence event_date record is matched to the claimed event (same event/period). Read literally ("any primary evidence record … shows the event date ≤ decision asof"), an unrelated past primary event in `primary_evidence` could CONTRADICT a legitimately future catalyst, and the §5.1 perturbation (single record, date moved to 2026-10-30) would not catch that false positive. The intended semantics is clear (FactRecord carries `period`/`claim_text` for alignment) and the fixtures contain exactly one evidence record, so tests won't trip. Smallest fix: add one sentence to §2.2(i) — evidence records are compared only after event/period identity matches the claim (e.g., `period == "FY2026-Q4"` for the MU claim).
