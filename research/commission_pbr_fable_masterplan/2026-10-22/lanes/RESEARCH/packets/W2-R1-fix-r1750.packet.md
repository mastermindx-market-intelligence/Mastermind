   `matched` (identity agrees with the fact), `other_identity` (record has a period that differs) and `unidentified` (record lacks
   `period`). Only `matched` participates in: the <= asof contradiction (`catalyst_already_published_before_asof`), the forward
   confirmation (`forward_catalyst_confirmed_by_primary`), the past-event confirmation (`past_event_confirmed_by_primary`) and
   `primary_dates_past_event_differently`.

   relative to `decision_asof`"). Today the <= asof branch runs before the claimed-vs-asof split, so a correct PAST claim (claimed

     CONTRADICTED `primary_dates_past_event_differently`; else → the item 5 rule.

     equivalent).
