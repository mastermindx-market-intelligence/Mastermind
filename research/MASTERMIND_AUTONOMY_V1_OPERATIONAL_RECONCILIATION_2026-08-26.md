## 4. Sol attention / escalation policy

Sol must not be triggered by every worker state change.

### Does not require Sol attention

- Worker claim/start when lawful and expected;
- routine progress;
- ordinary unit/CI failures inside delegated repair scope;
- mechanical same-carrier repair/retest;
- successful child Job completion that the active COO/root can consume;
- routine evidence publication.

### Requires Sol attention

- `BLOCKED` on a material dependency or production proof;
- `DECISION_REQUEST` for architecture, authority, scope, source-law, security, rights, no-rebuild, or major product-journey ambiguity;
- required scope widening beyond the accepted commission;
- canonical source disagreement the operator cannot lawfully reconcile;
- destructive/irreversible operation;
- named milestone bundle complete and requiring executive review;
- final/root result awaiting Sol acceptance;
- independent review exposes a material defect requiring executive adjudication.

### Chairman attention

Only when a gate is outside delegated Sol authority: material Chairman-intent change, native credential/device/OTP confirmation, material spend/rights/legal authority, destructive action requiring Chairman approval, or other explicitly Chairman-owned boundary.
