## 1. Four questions, four evaluations

| Question | Unit / target | Required baseline and independent evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Can the system reconstruct what was knowable? | Source occurrence / event-cutoff / derived claim | Original bytes and owner receipts; before/after availability, correction and rule clocks; independent replay oracle |
| Can it classify economic change correctly? | Issuer-event comparison, with its supporting/contradictory evidence | Two blinded domain reviewers, adjudicated disagreements, expected refusals; distinguish a reproducible class from an unobservable causal truth |
| Does it help an analyst? | Analyst-case task session | Randomized/counterbalanced existing workspace versus the same workspace plus What Changed; identical source access |
| Does it predict beyond existing information? | Independent issuer/event episode and prospective outcome | Static fundamentals, price/technical and theme baselines; time-forward testing; immutable features; appropriate dependence and multiple-test controls |

Do not promote a correct reconstruction into proof of useful classification, a useful summary into investment alpha, or a high backtest number into live authority.

### Proposed source/classification benchmark

Target 192 issuer-event comparison units: 96 development, 48 blinded validation and 48 untouched holdout, balanced across the difficult families and negative cases. Keep issuers disjoint between validation/holdout where feasible and record any unavoidable correlated supply-chain/event clusters. Use deterministic metadata selection within each family, fixed before labeling/outcomes; do not cherry-pick by price performance. Actual admissible counts and exclusions must be reported.

This is a proposed source-format/pilot benchmark, **not an investment backtest sample-size justification or a substitute for the primary G-C release sample**. Expand the independent confirmatory population until the ratified positive/label counts and power gates are met; do not claim the 48-case holdout satisfies those gates. Forty-eight clean cases cannot establish a sub-1% population error rate. Under an independent Bernoulli model, zero errors in n cases has a one-sided 95% upper error bound of `1 - 0.05^(1/n)`; dependence makes the effective sample smaller. About 299 independent error-free cases are needed for that bound to fall below 1%. Report uncertainty rather than implying that a small golden set proves universal reliability.

## 4. Reconstruction and classification gates

**Nonnegotiable deterministic gates:** all valid golden outputs byte-reproduce under canonical serialization; every numerical claim resolves to allowed owner evidence; no source/rule/correction clock after the requested system cutoff enters a replay; no identity or rights bypass; all forbidden-mutation tests fail. Zero violations is a release gate for the tested corpus, not a population-performance estimate.

Classification scoring records precision, recall, abstention, coverage and confusion by economic family and source format. Abstention is not scored as a correct detection merely because it is safe. Source-supported change and causal mechanism labels are graded separately. Two reviewers label from the contemporaneously admitted evidence without forward prices or detector suggestions. Disagreement is reported; consensus adjudication is not called an objective causal oracle.

Use the primary acceptance charter's proposed G-C policy: point precision at least 95%, recall at least 80%, dependence-aware 95% lower precision bound at least 85%, and at least 100 predicted-positive plus 100 adjudicated-positive issuer-event units, with effective clusters and underpowered status explicit. Its 80% reconstruction-coverage target applies only to the predeclared supported/evaluable universe. Zero critical timestamp/identity/rights violations remains required. This threshold must be approved before holdout access. Insufficient independent observations yields PARTIAL or human-reviewed scope, not a reduced threshold after seeing results. Product beta can remain bounded to independently checked supported issuers while prospective evidence accrues.

## 5. Analyst decision-value experiment

Use at least 12 analysts or qualified internal reviewers, with case order and treatment balanced; each participant sees each case only once. A proposed 24-case task set yields 288 sessions, but the analysis accounts for repeated analyst and issuer/event effects rather than treating every click as independent. Use an existing-workspace control with equal source access and a What Changed treatment. Blind outcome graders to condition where feasible.

Under the primary G-U charter, preregister **source-correct answer quality** as primary, with the proposed 10-percentage-point improvement over the strongest baseline and dependence-aware uncertainty excluding no improvement. Time to a correct evidence-supported answer is secondary; a fast wrong answer is a failure, not a speed win. Secondary endpoints: missed material changes, incorrect economic attribution, incorrect source-time claims, evidence retrieval time, useful falsifier identification and false-alert burden. The secondary target is at least 20% lower median correct-answer time without deterioration in material unsupported conclusions; this is a proposed business requirement, not a result. Freeze the noninferiority margin and analysis before sessions begin. Test missing/rights-blocked and conflicting-source cases, not only polished successes.

## 6. Prospective prediction design and bounded budget

Capture **outcome-blind immutable observations** as soon as the relevant producer is admitted. Store definitions and observation manifests in existing research governance, not a new ledger. Record exposure history, cutoff, input refs, missingness, rule hash, cohort, intended horizon and an outcome-not-yet-known state. Corrections append versions; do not overwrite the entry-time feature.

A proposed first experimental family has six constructions, each with one preregistered primary outcome and two nested comparisons. This is **12 planned primary comparisons**, not six. No experiments are registered merely by this document. The research owner must approve the complete manifest, family relationship to prior S10/IMCE trials and power analysis before data/outcome access.

| Construction | Proposed primary outcome | Core restriction |
|---|---|---|
| Demand acceleration with independent cash confirmation | Persistence of the specified demand proxy over the next two reported fiscal quarters | Definition and exact demand proxy fixed per admitted business family; no outcome-selected KPI |
| Revenue/receivables/cash contradiction | Subsequent owner-defined deterioration in cash conversion over two quarters | Tax, acquisitions, receivable sales and shrinking-business explanations remain controls |
| Margin recovery with durability gate | Persistence of comparable margin improvement over two quarters | New construction identity; no rerun of S10 v1; fiscal Q4 and vintage rules mandatory |
| Explicit guidance/commitment revision | Delivery against the revised, source-defined target at its deadline | Original and revised targets graded separately; bias/consistency not inferred from future outcomes |
| Financing-state deterioration | A predeclared adverse financing/liquidity event over 180 calendar days | Only Capital-accepted predicates/outcomes; authorization is not issuance; censor incomplete horizons |
| Composite economic transition as context | Incremental 63-trading-day market/sector-relative return prediction | No score export; only a registered research model; no directional trade authority |

For each construction compare (A) static financial levels plus price/technical/theme controls against the same model plus the named transition, and (B) a delta-only representation against a levels-plus-deltas representation. Specify the metric per outcome before registration: proper loss/calibration for probabilities; suitable continuous error/return statistic otherwise. No best-of-many unregistered horizons. Exploratory secondary endpoints are labeled and counted in the appropriate testing family, not quietly promoted to primaries.

Use rolling/expanding time-forward training and validation, preserving a final untouched period; keep issuers and overlapping economic episodes clustered, purge overlapping target windows and apply a horizon-appropriate embargo. Bootstrap shared calendar blocks/issuer clusters with aligned origins and real trading calendars. Report effective independent episodes, confidence intervals, minimum detectable effect and achieved precision. Freeze cohort/size/sector/regime cuts; model selection is part of the test budget. Underpowered results are inconclusive, not proof of absence or permission to promote.

Any investment implementation also needs delisting/selection analysis, corporate-action basis, time-to-executable observation, costs/spreads and optional borrow constraints. Source selection must include failures and inactive firms where available. Forward price data is an outcome, not source authority for whether a business changed. Controlled observation-only shadow can begin after separate technical/source/rights admission, without already-proven predictability; a positive historical result alone never grants promotion; a negative result does not invalidate descriptive user benefit.

## 8. Closeout law

Every validation return names the frozen method, source and output hashes, coverage/exclusions, all attempted configurations, passed and failed gates, confidence limits and what remains unproven. Expected refusals are demonstrated with forbidden mutations. A new natural event is not a production proof unless it traverses the actual admitted source → compiler → consumer path. A real live path is not predictive proof. Each feature earns its own verdict: ADMIT_SHADOW, HOLD_INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE, REJECT_CONSTRUCTION or later explicit promotion under Prophet's existing law.
