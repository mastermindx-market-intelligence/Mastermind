## Renewed thesis

**Complete a longitudinal, evidence-bearing issuer-change projection over existing financial, event, economic-interpretation and capital owners.** Do not build a second financial-analysis engine or economic-interpretation engine. Keep static economic levels alongside changes. The stronger claim that trajectory information generally outperforms static fundamentals is not established; incremental utility must be measured against credible static, simple-delta and source-cited-summary baselines. [Research review](RESEARCH_AND_RIGHTS.md).

The proposed `issuer_state_transition.v1` is conditional on W0's contract-fit decision. It may contain owner references, comparison eligibility, measured changes, support/contradiction and explicitly labeled hypotheses. It does not own financial facts, per-event interpretations, identity, rights, clocks or a new lifecycle. A current state needs an explicit **canonical baseline plus eligible deltas**, preserving unchanged, unavailable and unsupported variables; a change log alone cannot reconstruct an issuer's state.

The product is an existing-company-workspace **What Changed?** lens: concise trajectory, prior/current facts, exact evidence, operating versus accounting/disclosure/financing distinctions, contradictions, next discriminator and falsifier. It can be valuable without implying a trade.
