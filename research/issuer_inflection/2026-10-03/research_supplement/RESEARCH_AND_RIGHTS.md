# Research evidence, competitive workflows and data-rights admission

> **Same-program reconciliation:** this is a research/engineering supplement to the [audited masterplan](../../../../docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-03-issuer-inflection-intelligence-program.md) and [single acceptance charter](../VALIDATION_AND_ACCEPTANCE.md), not a competing wave ledger. Existing CDV-1 economic interpretation, canonical baseline state, owner-native rights and controlled evidence-collection shadow are preserved. Numerical experiment suggestions require one W0 ratification; they are not extra authorized trials or substitutes for the primary release gates.

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-03. **Evidence classes:** empirical research; research synthesis; statistical-method paper; official regulatory/data documentation; vendor-documented workflow; internal accepted experiment; our design inference. These are not interchangeable. Public product descriptions were reviewed, not paid terminals or customer license agreements. Scholarly claims below are limited to the inspected author/publisher abstracts and available HTML, not an asserted reproduction of the papers' data or tables.

## 1. Revised thesis and strongest objections

The useful thesis is **conditional incrementality**: source-grounded changes, accounting bridges and contradictions may improve an analyst's understanding beyond current levels alone. The product should retain both. The stronger claim that longitudinal transitions generally outperform static fundamentals in investment decisions is not established by this review.

Three objections materially change the build. First, static profitability has empirical relevance; a static baseline must not be designed to lose. Second, historical anomalies weaken under replication, more realistic samples and post-publication trading. Third, Mastermind's own S10 margin-turn construction failed its registered gate and did not correctly implement fiscal-quarter adjacency. A new label does not rehabilitate that experiment. [E2, E3, E4; internal I10 in the census]

Descriptive value can stand on correct reconstruction and lower analyst error even when return prediction is null. Economic interpretation remains a falsifiable claim, not deterministic source truth. Trading authority is a separate research and release decision.

## 2. Evidence review and resulting decisions

| Area and evidence | What it supports | What it does not establish / implementation consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Earnings quality: Chan et al., working paper 2001 / published 2006 [E1] | Historical association of accrual-heavy earnings increases with weaker subsequent returns; operating and cash-flow context matters. | Does not prove manipulation by an individual issuer or current alpha. Show accrual/cash divergence and alternatives, not fraud labels. |
| Static profitability: Novy-Marx, working paper 2010 / published 2013 [E2] | Gross profitability levels have demonstrated cross-sectional relevance. | Does not test this transition compiler. Always compare level-only, delta-only and combined models. |
| Replication: Hou, Xue and Zhang, 2017 working paper [E3] | In that version's 447-variable exercise, 286 were insignificant at the conventional level after reducing microcap influence; stronger thresholds rejected more. | Counts differ from later published versions. This packet uses the working-paper version only. Freeze the sample, universe and multiple-test family; do not infer universal absence of useful signals. |
| Publication decay: McLean and Pontiff, 2016 [E4] | The studied predictors weakened out of sample and after publication. | Does not forecast decay of a specific new feature. Demand untouched prospective performance and plausible implementation costs. |
| Accrual mechanism alternative: Wu, Zhang and Zhang, 2007/2008 [E5] | An investment-based interpretation can account for important accrual-return patterns. | Accruals are not automatically discretionary abuse. Maintain rational investment and timing explanations alongside earnings-quality concerns. |
| Asset growth: Cooper, Gulen and Schill, 2008 [E6] | Asset expansion is associated with subsequent returns in their historical cross-section. | 'More capex = bullish' is unjustified. Separate capacity commitments, utilization, cash burden and realized productivity. |
| Investment mechanism disagreement: Titman, Wei and Xie, 2013 [E7]; cash-flow-news study, 2015 online [E8] | The international study supports a q-theory interpretation; the cash-flow-news study supports expectational errors and challenges a risk-only explanation. | Preserve competing mechanisms. Do not convert an observational residual into a causal diagnosis. |
| PEAD measurement: Livnat and Mendenhall, 2006 [E9] | Analyst-based and time-series-based surprises can yield different drift patterns. | A seasonal earnings difference is not manufactured consensus. Give model-based expectation its own name and never fill a missing analyst baseline with it silently. |
| Information diffusion: Hirshleifer, Peng and Wang, 2023/revised 2024 [E10] | Public-news diffusion is associated with immediate reaction, later drift and trading behavior. | New economic information need not be unpriced. Keep incorporation separate from economic direction; price reaction is not proof of the mechanism. |
| Management forecasts: Hilary, Hsu and Wang, 2014 [E11] | Forecast consistency and forecast accuracy are different; predictable bias can still be informative. | Do not rank management solely by absolute forecast error or infer honesty from one beat. Preserve horizon, interval, conditions and the source-supported commitment. |
| Forecast-cycle literature review: Preussner and Aschauer, 2022 [E12] | Forecasting is a multiperiod interaction among management, analysts and investors; incentives, uncertainty and track records matter. | This is a synthesis, not one causal experiment. A future realized forecast error is unavailable at issuance. Classify it only once the outcome is observable. |
| Non-GAAP exclusions: Chen, 2010 [E13] | Persistence of excluded items and market understanding changed with the reporting regime in the studied sample. | 'Adjusted' is not synonymous with nonrecurring or comparable. FIF-7 must retain reconciliations and definition versions. |
| Filing novelty: Cohen, Malloy and Nguyen, 2018/revised 2019 [E14] | Changes in filing language were associated with future operating results and returns in the historical sample. | Textual novelty is not business onset. Reclassification, legal boilerplate, acquisitions and changed disclosure obligations are rival explanations. |
| Financial vocabulary: Loughran–McDonald author-hosted dictionary [E15] | Domain-specific language categories are preferable to unexamined generic sentiment. | The current downloadable version is not automatically PIT-safe. Commercial use requires licensing; dictionary version and negation/context remain material. |
| Online structural change: Adams and MacKay, 2007 [E16] | Online run-length inference supplies a formal changepoint framework under stated distributional assumptions. | A posterior conditional on a model is not calibrated probability of a real economic inflection. Use filtered, not future-smoothed, states at decision time. |
| SEC XBRL/disclosure documentation [E17–E19] | Source periods, original filings, KPI definitions and changed calculation methods matter. | Flattened extracted datasets are not complete substitutes for original evidence or semantic reconciliation. |

### Research priorities by economic family

**High priority for descriptive implementation:** guidance withdrawals/changes with exact baselines; reported growth and margin changes on comparable periods; cash-versus-earnings divergence; working-capital bridges; definition changes; source conflicts; financing-state consequences sourced from Capital Structure. These answer clear analyst questions without needing an expected-return claim.

**Sector-specific, not one global formula:** SaaS net retention, billings and remaining obligations; industrial backlog and cancellations; semiconductor utilization/inventory; homebuilder orders, incentives and communities; banks' deposit/funding, credit and net-interest economics. A metric can be excellent for one business and economically inapplicable to another.

**Research-only until qualified:** peer-residual attribution, narrative novelty measures, management consistency statistics, statistical changepoint probability, inferred capital efficiency and information-incorporation estimates. Each needs defined units, sufficient comparable observations and a validation target.

**Unavailable until rights/data admission:** historical consensus revisions, dispersion, historical options-implied uncertainty, vendor panel sell-through and private channel inventory. Missing expectations must not become a zero surprise or a narrative proxy mislabeled as consensus.

No independently validated general 'analyst pressure score' or issuer-wide commitment reliability score was established in this run. Structured Q&A and explicit promise/outcome comparisons are supportable descriptive work; pressure, evasiveness and intent remain hypotheses requiring role/source accuracy and blinded validation.

### Statistical architecture choice

Start with explicit fiscal-calendar deltas and evidence rules. Compare optional statistical candidates only after that baseline works. A local-level/local-trend state-space model can represent latent trend and measurement noise but may be weakly identified with short, revised quarterly series. A regime model additionally requires assumptions about number of states and transitions; do not name its hidden states 'recovery' or 'deterioration' without external labels. Multivariate models must account for correlated metrics and missingness rather than count correlated accounting equations as corroboration.

Use one-sided/online inference for detection-time claims. Retrospective segmentation is useful for research annotation but must be tagged hindsight. Hazard priors, seasonality treatment, likelihood, training window, minimum observations and model selection count are preregistered. A deterministic single-event guidance withdrawal is an observed commitment transition even if statistical persistence cannot yet be estimated. These are design recommendations, not new empirical findings.

**What Mastermind already does better?** No controlled head-to-head evidence establishes general superiority. Its inspected contracts explicitly preserve context-only authority, replay receipts, non-evaluability and narrow proof states. Those are internal strengths and useful product requirements, not evidence that competitors lack equivalent safeguards. The differentiator to earn is a useful, correction-safe cross-domain explanation with contradictions and falsifiers—not a claim that source links or AI comparison are unique.

## 4. Data availability and rights matrix

Every input admission must record source owner, issuer/event coverage, oldest/newest supported vintage, first-public and first-system clocks, latency distribution, normalization/version, correction behavior, and four distinct permissions: public display, internal use, model context, historical research. Unknown permission means blocked for that use. Purchasing or activating a paid feed is outside this research turn.

|---|---|---|---|
| Filing packages / XBRL | Accepted AAPL golden packages; FF broad current-quarter source capability historically proven; historical correction completeness unfinished | Filing acceptance and occurrence/record clocks are distinct. Original dimensions/units/periods and revisions remain necessary. No fresh broad latency measurement. | Public SEC access does not make every incorporated third-party exhibit unrestricted. Use current owner rights profiles; ordinary cited facts may be presented only through admitted product policy. Backtesting needs vintage reconstruction, not current CompanyFacts. |

LSEG I/B/E/S is a distinct important comparator, not subsumed into the other platforms. Adapt that two-clock distinction and analyst/target-period specificity. Do not repeat vendor exclusivity claims about being the only PIT source, and do not assume Mastermind has the associated commercial rights. [V16]

## 7. Research limits that become acceptance work

This review does not establish a reliable universal numerical threshold for an 'economic inflection,' a profitable universal margin detector, a causal financing-danger score, or a calibrated narrative-confidence model. It also does not establish current Mastermind ownership of historical consensus, options or channel panels. Rather than leave those as vague research tasks, the plan admits descriptive families first, proposes explicit bounded experiments, and makes source/rights/power shortfalls typed gates with named owners. A negative result can close a construction without closing the product's descriptive value.
