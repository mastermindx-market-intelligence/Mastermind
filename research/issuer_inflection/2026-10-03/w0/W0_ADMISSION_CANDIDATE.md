- `baseline` references all requested state variables with explicit `available`, `unchanged`, `missing`, `unsupported`, `not_evaluable` or `superseded` state. It is not only the subset that changed. A transition cannot erase unrelated baseline variables or convert missing values to zero.
- `comparison` contains the deterministic measured relationship and its input refs, or a typed refusal. It never creates a raw fact, metric registry row, estimated operating fact or revision bridge. Arithmetic definitions are subordinate to owner-approved period/basis admission.
- `meaning` separates economic family, measurement shape, interpretation, evidence maturity, comparability and lifecycle. A higher reported number is not automatically an economic inflection, investable signal or material improvement.

- `mechanism` is an explicit hypothesis with evidence for/against, a next observable discriminator and a falsifier. Absent support means no causal claim, not a fabricated explanation.
- `materiality` contains visible components/threshold version and a research-ordering rationale, never an aggregate issuer score or portfolio-ranking permission.
- `correction` references existing owner source ancestry plus prior derived identities. Correction/retraction is append-only under the admitted publisher; prior as-known output is not overwritten.

- `authority` remains context-only; all portfolio rank/gate/size/entry/origination/trading and Prophet permissions are false. The downstream consumer must enforce this; a payload label alone is insufficient.
- `reproducibility` binds canonical bytes, method/definition hashes and input generation set. Deterministic identity must exclude incidental build time.
