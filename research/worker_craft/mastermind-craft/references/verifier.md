# Production verifier: prove the real journey

## Input and output

Input: an exact accepted release/candidate, approved target and principal, real
input, required journey, and existing evidence/rollback owners. Output: attributable
real-path evidence or a concrete failed/unavailable gate. Verification grants no
new mutation, deployment, browser, credential, or account authority.

## Method

1. Identify the actual target, source/release relation, account/project coverage,
   current host generation, data time/state, and scope of any permitted effects.
   Do not select the newest tab, a similarly named document, or a convenient server.
2. Distinguish installed, connected, authenticated, callable, authorized, and useful.
   A ping or health endpoint proves only its own narrow condition. Call the actual
   consumer with a real permitted input and retain its serialized result.
3. Follow the primary user/machine journey end to end. For visual work, capture and
   inspect actual screenshot bytes with viewport, theme, language and data state.
   A screenshot path, successful export, or synthetic HTML fixture is not that proof.
4. Exercise required failure/partial/correction behavior and one practical recovery
   path through existing owners. Record exactly what was not exercised. Do not
   manufacture a live fault, logout, production write, or destructive rollback without
   the separate authorization and scope required for that action.
5. Reconcile every attempted side effect before closing. Preserve ambiguous outcomes
   on the original carrier. Do not replay a lost action, switch hosts, or borrow
   another authenticated realm to make the proof green.
6. Record evidence with source/build, target, timestamps, artifact hashes, observation
   scope, and limits. Keep private captures in the approved artifact home; publish
   only an authorized non-secret index. Use existing Reality/evidence procedures.

## Deliverable

Return the capability delta, observed journey, exact evidence pointers, negative
proof, unresolved states, and next dependency. Use PROVEN_LIVE only within the exact
validated environment and scope; the runtime/acceptance owner remains authoritative.
Never average a successful component into a claim that the whole fleet works.

## Stop or escalate

Stop at unavailable auth, wrong target/build, insufficient rights, unclear effects,
or missing mandatory capture/consumer evidence. Report the exact gate. Do not turn
inspection into unauthorized installation, deployment, account enrollment, or repair.
