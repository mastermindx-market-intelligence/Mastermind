# 01 — Executive Decision, Forensic Census, and Evidence Synthesis

## Executive decision

**Final recommendation: CONTINUE — NARROWED.**

**FACT —** The prior Trend Persistence program has already falsified two important formulations. At the stock level, the path-efficiency / gain-retention / drawdown-shape family added negligible incremental information beyond a strong return, beta, and volatility baseline. Those exact constructions are closed. At the group level, the single preregistered eleven-sector C1 development run produced C1-NULL; none of its gated cells carried. That exact eleven-sector construction is closed and there is no lawful C2 read.

**FACT —** Formation dates 2022-07-06 through 2026-06-02 have already been inspected repeatedly by V2/B2/C1 and are not fresh confirmatory evidence. Bootstrap, cross-validation, relabeling, or a new feature name cannot make those observations independent again.

**STRONG INFERENCE —** The next scientifically worthwhile question is not “can another technical persistence score predict raw future return?” It is:

> Among stocks that are leaders now, does leave-issuer-out evidence that economically related peers are also durable leaders predict the probability that the focal stock remains a leader, or avoids a material leadership break, beyond a strong own-stock momentum / residual-momentum / volatility baseline?

The recommended first successor is a **hybrid of Strategy C and Strategy A**:

1. Make **leadership-state survival** and **trend-break / state-exit hazard** the primary outcome family.
2. Use **GICS industry group** as the primary static grouping, with industry as a prespecified secondary level only when peer count is sufficient.
3. Test only a small frozen family of **independent peer continuity, breadth/diffusion, peer residual strength, and leader-dependency / concentration** predictors.
4. Exclude the focal issuer and alternate listings from every peer statistic.
5. Require a **group-only comparator** and the full stock-only / group-only / additive / interaction decomposition.
6. Adjudicate on **genuinely untouched evidence**: preferably an independent international historical equity panel with point-in-time industry classifications if lawful entitlement exists; otherwise a prospective post-freeze U.S. cohort.

This is materially different from the killed predecessors. B2 asked whether the focal stock's own price-path shape added information beyond momentum and volatility. C1 asked whether eleven coarse sector aggregates improved future SPY-relative returns. The successor asks whether **independent peers in a finer economic group predict state transitions of an already-leading stock**.

### Target hierarchy

| Priority | Target | Ruling |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Leadership-state survival | Best match to the decision problem; directly estimable and interpretable |
| Co-primary mechanism | Trend-break / state-exit hazard | Captures when failure risk rises and handles time-to-event / censoring |
| Secondary | Forward market-/group-relative return | Necessary economic interpretation, but should not define persistence |
| Secondary | MAE, recovery time/probability | Holding-path diagnostics only after incremental survival information exists |
| Later only | Expected holding utility | Build only from separately validated components; do not invent a composite now |

**HYPOTHESIS —** Conditional on a stock's own relative strength, residual momentum, beta, and volatility, **higher leave-issuer-out peer continuity plus broad peer participation in a point-in-time industry group increases 20- and 60-session leadership survival and lowers leadership-break hazard.**

That is the claim Experiment DL-1 should be designed to kill.

## External evidence synthesis

The literature gives a strong prior for medium-horizon continuation and slow information diffusion, but a much weaker prior that any particular price-shape or group score remains incremental after strong controls.

### Strong positive evidence

**FACT — Cross-sectional and time-series momentum are real background phenomena.** International and cross-asset evidence supports medium-horizon continuation. This establishes the background phenomenon, not a new Mastermind edge.

**FACT — Industry and economic-link evidence is real.** Moskowitz and Grinblatt found strong industry momentum. Hou found that big-to-small lead-lag is predominantly intra-industry and linked it to slow diffusion of industry information. Cohen and Frazzini found that public information about principal customers predicts subsequent supplier returns.

**FACT — Information-arrival structure matters.** Gradual information diffusion, analyst coverage, and post-earnings-announcement drift evidence support underreaction / limited-attention mechanisms.

**FACT — Institutional demand and short/option information can matter.** Fund herding, short activity, and options-implied information have predictive evidence in particular settings, but these sources introduce timing, rights, and interpretation complexity.

### Strong negative / competing evidence

**FACT — Industry effects are not the only explanation for momentum.** Grundy and Martin found strong stock-specific momentum after controls.

**FACT — Common factor momentum is a serious confounder.** Ehsani and Linnainmaa show that momentum in high-eigenvalue common factors can subsume much individual-stock momentum. A “group confirmation” feature that merely reconstructs a common factor is not incremental stock-selection information.

**FACT — Volume confirmation is ambiguous.** Lee and Swaminathan found volume predicts momentum magnitude/persistence, but high-volume winners can reverse faster over longer horizons.

**FACT — Momentum is regime-dependent and can crash.** Daniel and Moskowitz show crash risk is concentrated in particular rebound / high-volatility states. Regime should therefore be an interaction axis, not a point contribution to an opaque score.

### Evidence interpretation

The literature supports a **plausible mechanism prior**, not permission to skip strong controls. A successful Mastermind result must show incremental information beyond own-stock momentum, residual momentum, volatility, broad-sector state, and common-factor momentum. It must also distinguish group timing from stock selection by carrying the group-only comparator.

The most important adversarial interpretation is:

> If group-only predicts the environment but stock+group does not improve the stock-only baseline, the evidence supports group timing/context, not stock-selection durability.
