## 3. CEO ruling

### Internal review draft

A repaired Canva draft may display unresolved price observations only when all of the following remain true:

- the artifact is access-controlled and labelled `INTERNAL REVIEW DRAFT — NOT FOR DISTRIBUTION`;
- the unresolved rows carry `internal_only` or `unknown` truth rather than silently appearing cleared;
- no public link, export, client delivery, marketing use or paid-user publication occurs;
- source, observation date, session basis and retrieval/verification date remain visible;
- selected sessions are described as selected examples, never a complete or unbiased return series;
- visual inspection and Chairman approval concern the draft content only and do not waive the rights gate.

### External or public release

The report remains `RIGHTS_BLOCKED`. External release is prohibited while any emitted field, chart, image, font or design asset has a rights class of `unknown`, `internal_only` or `do_not_redistribute`, or lacks an entitlement receipt covering the exact distribution channel.

For this report, the load-bearing unresolved family is market-price history currently verified through StockAnalysis.com. The site identifies Cboe and Nasdaq UTP among its price providers, while its terms permit only unmodified, attributed snippets absent explicit permission. The planned report does not merely quote an unmodified snippet: it selects, calculates, relabels and visually transforms observations. StockAnalysis access therefore does not prove Mastermind's right to distribute the transformed price data or derived charts.

Cboe separately publishes internal, external and digital-media licensing categories for market-data distribution. The existence of a public web display is not a Mastermind entitlement receipt. No such receipt was found on PR #524 or in the Canva evidence package.

**Required resolution:** replace the website-derived price lane with output from the canonical Market Data owner carrying an accepted source/entitlement receipt for the intended channel, or obtain and retain an explicit licence/permission that covers the exact values, transformations and distribution scope. Attribution alone does not convert an unresolved licence into a cleared one.

## 5. Artifact-level source envelope

Every externally emitted quantitative or documentary fact must resolve to one source envelope owned by the canonical evidence/data plane. The Canva population package may project the envelope but must not mint a competing identity.

Minimum fields:

- `source_system`
- `source_id` or canonical evidence ID
- `canonical_url` or stable retained-source locator
- issuer/security identity
- observation/effective date
- `as_of`, `knowable_at`, `captured_at` and `retrieved_at` where applicable
- session/time-zone basis for market observations
- nominal/adjusted/corporate-action basis
- source vendor and product
- `rights.redistribution_class`
- `rights.attribution_required`
- `rights.license_note`
- licensed distribution scope: internal, client, public web, downloadable export or other exact channel
- calculation recipe and input evidence IDs for derived fields
- correction/revision state and superseded-source pointer
- rendered attribution text and destination link

A missing field remains null with a named reason. It must not default to public, current, unadjusted, complete, zero or licensed.

## 7. Guard extension to the existing publishing contract

This attaches to the guarded publishing contract already specified in R1; it does not create another service.

External publication must fail closed unless all are true:

1. content status is the accepted report revision;
2. exact native design revision was read back after the approved commit;
3. full-size visual QA passed on the saved revision;
4. every emitted source envelope is present and correction-current;
5. every emitted source has a distribution class permitted for the requested channel;
6. no source or asset remains `unknown`, `internal_only` or `do_not_redistribute`;
7. every required attribution and source link is rendered and legible;
8. every Canva/non-first-party asset has retained licence evidence;
9. the explicit publication authority approves that exact saved revision and channel.

A Canva share link, downloaded PDF, connector receipt, successful export operation, green CI run or Chairman approval of a preview is not by itself a rights-clearance receipt.

## 8. Discriminating negative tests

The publication gate must hold or fail visibly when:

- StockAnalysis values are present but no upstream redistribution receipt exists;
- the source is attributed but the transformation/distribution scope is still unlicensed;
- a Cboe or UTP product is named but the subscriber/distributor and channel are absent;
- an internal-use entitlement is presented for a public website or client PDF;
- a rights state is null and silently defaults to public;
- a vendor licence expires after the draft was produced but before publication;
- a corrected market bar supersedes the value rendered in Canva;
- one chart combines observations with different nominal/adjusted bases;
- a Mastermind calculation has no resolvable input evidence IDs;
- an issuer press-release photo or Canva stock element has no retained asset licence;
- NRC text is government-authored but an embedded third-party graphic is not;
- a public source link exists but rendered attribution is clipped or unreadable;
- a report is copied to a new delivery channel not covered by the retained licence;
- preview approval is incorrectly treated as publication approval;
- removal of exact price labels leaves a visual whose geometry still implies unlicensed quantitative magnitude.
