### F03 - The source clock normalization loses uncertainty

The date-only branch returns a midnight-UTC timestamp based on string length/separators and does not validate the calendar date on that branch. Naive timestamps are assigned UTC. Some leg readers substitute composition time for missing source time. Those behaviors may be convenient for display formatting, but they do not satisfy C19's point-in-time availability contract. The repaired projection must retain precision, timezone evidence and a separate serving clock. [S06]

### F04 - The relative freshness test can miss uniformly stale input

Future source clocks are not refused by this composition loop. Source-specific policy and upstream validators must be considered before deciding the exact repair. Our isolated probe reproduces the loop behavior with synthetic supplied legs, not a live user incident. [S06; verification/]
