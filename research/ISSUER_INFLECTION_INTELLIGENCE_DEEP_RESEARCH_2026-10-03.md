## 1. Verdict and renewed thesis

The original report is a useful architectural sketch, **not a research-resolved completion blueprint**. Its strongest decisions survive: change-first intelligence, owner-native facts, correction safety, explicit absence, no opaque issuer score, and separate predictive authority. Its census and construction instructions do not survive unchanged.

The most consequential omission is existing **Consumer Defensive CDV-1** work. Mastermind already contains a private P&G economic-observation validator and a deterministic `earnings.economic_interpretation/v1` compiler. These have an active owner, frozen tests, source/publication gates and substantive comparison semantics. Rebuilding them under an Issuer Inflection namespace would duplicate both implementation and authority. The audit reproduced **427 passing interpretation tests** against pinned source in an isolated snapshot; that is source-level reuse evidence, not production or cross-issuer proof. [I5–I7]

The renewed thesis is therefore conditional:

> Mastermind should complete a longitudinal, evidence-bearing issuer-change **projection over existing financial, event, economic-interpretation and capital owners**. Its incremental value must be demonstrated against a static financial view, simple deterministic deltas and a source-cited summary. It must distinguish a change in economic conditions from a change in reporting, expectations, financing or available evidence. Predictive investment value is a separate empirical question.

The missing capability is a combination of incomplete source/history availability, owner-side comparability and convergence, cross-event composition, product delivery and validation. It is **not** an entirely missing economic interpretation engine, and it is **not** merely a join over a finished data substrate.

A useful descriptive product need not predict returns to justify itself. Conversely, reconstructing a known winner or passing a source validator does not establish analyst usefulness or alpha. A well-conducted negative or underpowered predictive study is a valid research disposition; it cannot excuse unfinished engineering.

A module that mainly abstains, repeats a release or labels hindsight winners does not pass H1.

## 7. Economic ontology and operational definition

An inflection is a **qualified change in the evidence-backed description of an issuer's economic trajectory**, not a synonym for any changed number. Continuous metrics and discrete events require different rules. Preserve five orthogonal axes:

1. **Family:** demand; price/mix; growth; profitability; working capital/cash; investment/capacity; financing; management/commitment; accounting/reporting; expectations/market context.
2. **Measurement shape:** level change; growth change; acceleration/deceleration; structural-break candidate; no measured change.
3. **Interpretation:** recovery, deterioration, reacceleration, accounting translation, mixed or unresolved, each with a rule/definition reference.
4. **Evidence/comparability:** observed/candidate/corroborated/contradicted; comparable/bridged/not comparable/not evaluable, with reasons.
5. **Record lifecycle:** current, superseded by correction, retracted or expired for current-use purposes. Historical validity is not erased.

| Family | Useful dimensions | Mandatory confounders / limits |
|---|---|---|
| Demand/activity | Units, orders, bookings, backlog, utilization, churn, sell-through | Sell-in is not sell-through; backlog cancellations, duration and denominator changes matter. |
| Price/mix | ASP, price contribution, mix, ARPU/take rate | Price/volume/mix bridges must be source-defined; combined volume/mix cannot be split by invention. |
| Growth | Revenue, organic/segment growth, guidance | Fiscal calendar, FX, acquisitions, divestitures, segment and KPI definitions. |
| Profitability | Gross/operating margin, contribution/incremental margin | Revenue denominator, GAAP/non-GAAP basis, fixed-cost absorption and one-off costs. |
| Working capital/cash | Receivables, inventory, payables, cash conversion, CFO/FCF | Average versus end balances; matched periods; acquisitions; financing cash flows; sector applicability. |
| Investment/capacity | Capex, R&D, hiring, capacity and utilization | Maintenance/growth split only if supported; spending is not demonstrated return on investment. |
| Financing | Liquidity, maturities, authorization, issuance, dilution and funding need | Capital Structure owns calculations; authorization is not issuance, capacity is not cash raised. |
| Management | Commitments, guidance changes, delivery, source-linked topic/Q&A changes | Changed wording can be formatting, role/coverage loss or legal language rather than changed intent. |
| Reporting | Definitions, segments, policies, revisions, non-GAAP reconciliations | Reporting change may make an economic comparison unevaluable without invalidating the source observation. |
| Expectations/market | Lawful vintage consensus, dispersion, reactions, implied uncertainty | Rights, timestamps, security identity, trading session and incorporation are separate from operating truth. |

Materiality is a visible vector, not a weighted black box: magnitude, persistence, novelty, independent corroboration, comparability, cash/capital consequence, commitment consequence and freshness. Estimated dimensions must identify their method and uncertainty. A missing component stays missing; it is not set to zero to make sorting work.

## 8. Clock, correction and composition decisions

**No new clock authority.** Reference the existing owners' period, publication, availability, capture/admission and correction timestamps. Add only derived clock semantics and precision metadata in the projection.

For a fixed rule and a sufficient supporting input set S, earliest support time cannot precede `max(availability(input) for input in S)`. When alternative sufficient sets exist, choose the earliest valid set under the frozen selection policy and record it; a cherry-picked early fragment cannot stand in for the full claim. Label this **earliest supported within the examined corpus**, not the earliest moment any person could have known anything about the business. System-as-known time also respects actual admission and method availability. Emission time is when the system actually emitted; retrospective reconstruction must never impersonate a historical alert.

Date-only, timezone-ambiguous and interval timestamps must not become fictitious exact instants. Operating onset may be unknown or interval-valued. A model's retrospective estimate of onset is not disclosure time. An offline changepoint's estimated break and detection time are separate fields.

Corrections create new owner-backed revisions and new derived generations, retaining the prior as-known view. Distinguish source correction, restatement/recast, parser correction, identity-map correction, method change and rights revocation. Later knowledge cannot silently rewrite earlier evaluation inputs. Cache invalidation must follow both source revisions and rights changes.

**Representation decision:** preserve `event_workspace.v1` and `earnings.economic_interpretation/v1` unchanged. A new `issuer_state_transition.v1` is justified only for the additional cross-event/cross-owner relationship, evidence grouping, comparison definition, correction ancestry and derived clocks that the existing closed contracts cannot safely carry. W0 must ratify that fit decision and its owning workstream. Use existing authority vocabulary (`context_only`) and consumer enforcement, not a novel enum that an existing validator silently ignores.

An issuer snapshot is a rebuildable query projection over **baseline owner references + eligible transitions + explicit unchanged/missing state**. Derived persistence may reuse the existing immutable publication owner and its storage conventions. It must not become a second financial fact database, event store, metric registry, identity registry, peer graph or outcome/control plane.

## 9. Rights and data admission

Public accessibility is not a universal license for redistribution, embedding, training or retention. The source owner must supply the accepted rights decision for internal analysis, model context, public display, machine export, storage and correction/withdrawal behavior. The projection's permissions are the intersection of its input permissions and consumer authorization.

SEC filing packages must preserve exact source versions; SEC Company Facts and Frames are not interchangeable with filing-package semantics or historical as-known snapshots. SEC's API documentation describes Frames selecting last-filed facts fitting calendar periods, which is precisely why a current Frames response cannot be presumed historical PIT evidence. KPI definition/calculation changes and non-GAAP basis differences require source-aware comparability. [R14–R16]

P&G private inputs stay private until CDV-1 and the shared foundation's rights/publication gates actually clear. PR #7870 remains a live dependency, not a template to copy. Estimates remain unavailable until an owner proves licensed vintage history and corrections. Missing expectations must not suppress a valid descriptive observation or manufacture a beat/miss.

## 10. Product, validation and commissioning conclusion

The first user job is to open an issuer and answer **what changed since a chosen prior cutoff, why it matters, what cuts against the interpretation, and what to monitor next**. The first screen needs concise change cards, prior/current values and units, clock/maturity badges and one-click evidence. The second layer supplies mechanism alternatives, correction history and peer context. No opaque score is needed.

The same immutable transition identity and admitted evidence must reach the event comparator, dossier, research inbox, saved view/watchlist context, alerts and Ask/Neural Web. These are consumers, not competing recomputations. Watchlist saves use the existing owner; alerts use the existing lifecycle and subscription owner. Actual source availability, entitlement, read generation and emitted event identity must be demonstrated at release.

The full [masterplan](../docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-03-issuer-inflection-intelligence-program.md) makes owner completion, early product proof, validation capture and conditional shadow explicit. The [validation contract](issuer_inflection/2026-10-03/VALIDATION_AND_ACCEPTANCE.md) defines falsifiers, test denominators and release criteria. **The first commission is W0 current-delta recovery and owner/contract admission, followed by the bounded AAPL W1 vertical—not an immediate universal issuer engine or a new research run.**
