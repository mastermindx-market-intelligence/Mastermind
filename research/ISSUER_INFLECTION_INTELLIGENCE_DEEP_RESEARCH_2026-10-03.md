## Research evidence

Academic evidence supports treating cash-flow quality, reporting quality, information diffusion and structural breaks as meaningful context, but it does not support jumping to a universal return-predictive score.

- Chan, Chan, Jegadeesh and Lakonishok show why earnings changes require accrual/cash-flow context.
- PEAD and gradual-information research support separating **economic change** from **market incorporation**.
- Bayesian/structural change-point methods are useful candidate generators, but issuer histories are short and contaminated by basis changes, acquisitions, seasonality and disclosure timing.
- Replication research is a strong warning: many attractive anomalies weaken or fail under uniform methods and multiple-testing controls.

Required authority sequence:
1. correct point-in-time reconstruction;
2. valid state classification;
3. analyst decision usefulness;
4. prospective fundamental/expectation prediction;
5. only then separately validated return-predictive authority.

## Economic-state ontology

The state vector remains multidimensional:

1. demand/activity;
2. pricing/monetization/mix;
3. growth trajectory;
4. profitability/operating leverage;
5. working capital/cash conversion;
6. investment/capacity;
7. balance sheet/financing;
8. narrative/management behavior;
9. accounting/reporting structure;
10. expectations/market incorporation where rights and PIT history permit.

No family silently substitutes for another. Narrative is not a financial fact. Price action is not economic truth. A filing fact does not establish management intent.

## Multiple clocks

Represent separately:
- operating/economic;
- accounting/reporting;
- guidance/commitment;
- disclosure/publication;
- financing/capital structure;
- analyst/expectations;
- market price/volatility;
- Mastermind observation/admission/correction.

first_knowable_at means the earliest accepted public evidence timestamp at which the claimed transition could lawfully have been inferred under frozen rules. It is not period-end, a later ingestion timestamp, recomputation time, or today's reconstructed understanding.

## Definition of an inflection

An issuer inflection is a point-in-time-evaluable transition in an economically meaningful state variable that survives source, basis and comparability checks; is distinguishable from seasonality/noise or presentation change; and has sufficient magnitude, persistence, novelty, corroboration, commitment consequence or structural significance to change the best evidence-backed description of trajectory.

Transition vocabulary:
LEVEL_SHIFT; ACCELERATION; DECELERATION; STRUCTURAL_BREAK_CANDIDATE; CONFIRMATION; CONTRADICTION; REVERSAL; REACCELERATION; DETERIORATION; RECOVERY; DEFINITION_CHANGE; ACCOUNTING_TRANSLATION; TEMPORARY_VOLATILITY; UNRESOLVED; NOT_EVALUABLE.

A single quarter is a candidate, not automatically an inflection.

## Materiality without a score

Use a transparent vector:
magnitude; acceleration; novelty; persistence; independent breadth; source confidence; comparability; peer rarity; management commitment delta; expectation delta; corroboration; contradiction; cash/capital consequence; strategic consequence; market incorporation; freshness.

This vector may drive research priority and filters. It must not become bullishness, expected return, sizing, gating, or a 0–100 “Issuer Inflection Score.”

## Mechanism, contradiction and falsifiers

Every material transition should support:
- mechanism hypothesis;
- evidence for;
- evidence against;
- plausible alternatives;
- next observable discriminator;
- falsifier.

Generated prose may explain accepted evidence but cannot become source authority.
