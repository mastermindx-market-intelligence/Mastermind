# Open masterplan: one useful, recoverable AI organization

**Status:** Proposed synthesis and delivery charter, prepared for Fable. Existing protected law, original acceptance contracts, live source custody and runtime admission remain controlling. This is a companion to the existing ecosystem and Meta-CEO plans, not a new task registry or authority grant.

## 1. The product to finish

The Chairman should be able to speak to one dependable office, ask a strategic question, start or revise a permitted mission, leave, and return to a truthful account of what changed. The organization should remember relevant decisions, find and reuse existing work, distribute execution economically, resolve ordinary questions itself, reject superficially convincing but incomplete output, and recover from interruptions without duplicating effects.

The product is not merely a chat UI, a daemon, a model endpoint, a worker-launch tool, or a dashboard of PRs. It is the joined capability across all of them. Equally, it is not an unconstrained system that constantly invents work. When no useful authorized action exists, it should wait without burning reasoning or widening its mandate.

The user-visible promise can be expressed as four verbs: **understand, act, verify, remember**. Each has an existing owner and a real proof requirement:

| Verb | User benefit | Proof that matters |
|---|---|---|
| Understand | The right project, latest correction and original intent are recovered | A fresh session identifies existing accepted work and refuses a plausible duplicate or stale interpretation |
| Act | Ordinary permitted work progresses without Chairman message shuttling | The correct admitted worker consumes a bounded commission and later a real ordinary ruling |
| Verify | Completion means the requested outcome, not an agent's claim | An independent reviewer examines artifacts and rejects an intentionally incomplete alternative; the real result meets its consumer test |
| Remember | A lost chat does not lose accountability or effects | Existing records recover the same objective, original operation, accepted work and exact next action after an interruption |

A compelling office must also be responsive: it can answer an ordinary question while a project runs, explain a block without pretending everything is idle, and tell the user which single reserved decision genuinely needs attention.

- The first real mission, sequencing of independent work, release packaging, experiments, prototypes and implementation decomposition.
- Which proposed features to defer, simplify or reject when they do not improve the original product outcome.
- Performance targets after measuring a baseline, as long as original acceptance and real safety obligations are not weakened to produce a pass.

The design below is a strong starting hypothesis, not a script. Ordinary reversible in-scope decisions do not require another Chairman design-signoff. Changes to reserved scope, spend, authority or existing accepted requirements retain their genuine gates.

## 3. Thesis and strongest alternative

**Recommended thesis:** Complete one narrowly scoped governed office loop through the incumbent control plane, while independently qualifying the best resident interaction harness. Use that first loop to reveal the true bottlenecks. Then add concurrent missions, native research, Secretary capacity and reliability evidence.

**Strongest alternative:** Finish a standalone Hermes or DSH assistant first, use it productively in an advisory/read-only role, and only then integrate the full executive path. This can produce earlier conversational value and isolate harness usability. Its risk is another attractive assistant disconnected from company outcomes, with pressure to recreate memory, scheduling or delegation because integration feels slow.

Fable may choose the alternative if it is explicitly a limited release with owner-bound reads and no disguised autonomous-company claim. The two approaches can proceed in parallel on disjoint surfaces. Neither requires completing every infrastructure project before a real user benefit.

**What would reverse the recommendation:** Evidence that the existing admission/return chain cannot be safely qualified within the current authorized environment, while a narrower read-only office can prove a valuable Chairman workflow immediately. That changes the next product slice, not the canonical architecture or full completion definition.

## 4. Recommended first genuine mission

Choose an existing, useful, reversible need whose outcome is observable and whose scope avoids live custody conflicts. One suitable category is a bounded documentation/source-quality repair or a small approved product defect with a deterministic test and independent review. The responsible operator should select the actual task from the existing program; do not fabricate a production write simply to exercise a tool.

The journey should include:

1. The Chairman gives a direction in the actual supported Office. The principal recovers relevant prior decisions and shows a concise interpretation.
2. The existing authority/admission owner records the permitted work once, with original context and audience. A provider conversation ID is not substituted for the canonical operation.
3. An eligible operator/worker executes with its admitted source, tools, budget and effect limits. The Office remains available for another ordinary question.
4. The worker asks one naturally arising design or integration question. The appropriate principal decides; the exact originating worker demonstrably consumes the answer and resumes. A Slack post alone does not establish this.
5. A non-author reviewer tests the original outcome and actual artifacts. A planted or separately prepared plausible-but-incomplete alternative is rejected in a controlled evaluation, not slipped into production.
6. The original parent consumes the actual disposition and updates its existing records. The Office shows the result, limitations and next responsibility without cross-session attribution.
7. Interrupt the client connection at a qualified boundary and reconcile the same original request. No second submission, false cancellation, lost ruling or new root appears.

This proves the first office slice and some H/R2 obligations. H1 additionally requires its original multi-child/Fabric structure; native Project Chat, desktop packaging and the full campaign remain separate. Do not redefine a source-only test as this installed journey.

A general company completion claim, however, must still account for the original required browser/native capabilities.

## 7. Delivery organization and economics

Fable retains product thesis, integration decisions, contradictory-evidence adjudication and final outcome accountability. It should avoid becoming the routine test runner or relayer of every child transcript.

A useful domain partition is: office/product; runtime/return; harness/tool integration; Secretary/browser/continuity; independent assurance. This is a **menu**, not five mandatory teams. A qualified admitted operator may cover several compatible domains. Add a Sol/Opus coordinator only when the actual program judgment warrants it. Use economical qualified Fabric workers for bounded implementation and research, with review and repair capacity reserved before increasing fanout.

Worker suitability must be based on the child task, not the prestige of the parent. Provider, model, subscription, host, role and source writer are separate dimensions. Current pool quotas and actual allowed routes must be observed; this pack does not assert that a remembered model, account or Mini is available. Preserve current exclusions, approved spending envelopes and the original conserved root. Never manufacture depth or budget by opening another root.

Optimization target: **accepted outcome per total completion-cycle cost**, including framing, context retrieval, coordinator time, worker consumption, review, repair, integration and recoverable failure. Report actual provider usage when available; unknown billing is unknown, not zero. Included subscriptions still have scarcity and latency. Unlimited retry or hidden on-demand credits are not a strategy.

## 9. Non-goals and anti-failure rules

Do not build another executive database, Agent OS task store, universal memory warehouse, scheduler, model/account pool, browser estate, retry layer, identity plane, release authority or acceptance service. Subordinate harness transcripts and private caches may exist, but must not become company truth.

Do not equate a merged PR with installation, a launch attempt with provider reasoning, a terminal worker with accepted output, a delivered ruling with consumption, a seven-day schedule with seven days of observed reliability, or a fresh chat with a transferred lease.

Do not spend the program on exhaustive comparative research after a decision is sufficiently supported. Do not impose a fixed agent count, model brand, front-end layout, microtask sequence, deadline, or fabricated budget. Do not treat an old chat's absence as a permanent ownership gate. Do not ask the Chairman to solve ordinary placement, source repair or log archaeology.

## 10. Final acceptance and maintenance

The integrated outcome closes only when the responsible independent acceptance process has consumed the original intent, all required proof rows and actual installed behavior. A narrower release may be accepted in its declared scope without marking the parent complete. Remaining native, scientific, duration, security, private-data and recovery obligations remain named with their original owners.

After acceptance, maintain a small set of real regression journeys, source-linked release evidence, recoverable continuity and meaningful service health. Reassess models and harness assumptions when evidence changes, not merely when a new brand appears. The organization should become easier to operate as it matures, not accumulate an ever-growing approval and orchestration bureaucracy.
