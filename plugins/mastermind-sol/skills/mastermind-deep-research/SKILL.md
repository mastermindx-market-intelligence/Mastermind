# Research a Decision, Not a Bibliography

## Mandatory current-source gate

Run `bootstrap-mastermind` for the task. Read protected Mastermind `master`, record its exact revision, load `docs/sol_skills/INDEX.md` and required companions from that same exact commit, and verify compatibility. Reuse the verified pin and relevant procedures already loaded in the current turn while valid; new-turn recovery must re-establish current source. Material changes use the existing reconciliation procedure. If the read or compatibility fails, modifying workflow is unavailable; bounded read-only analysis may continue with explicit uncertainty.

Read `../../references/authority-boundaries.md` before interpreting any app, record, or action as authority. This skill grants no authority. Current Chairman intent, protected source law and platform permissions govern. GitHub owns source and evidence; existing operational owners retain state, custody and execution.

## Do not use

Do not expand a supplied-text summary, translation or settled factual lookup into deep research. Research permission does not authorize trading, purchases, implementation or access to restricted sources.

## Working method

1. Define the decision being informed, scope, horizon, consumers and what evidence would change the action. Separate research, forecasts and execution authority. State a few competing hypotheses before accumulating sources; include an explanation under which the favored thesis is wrong.
2. Prefer relevant primary sources and current direct evidence. Record event date separately from publication date, source scope and observation time. Verify changing facts rather than relying on memory. Trace recycled reports to their origin so ten echoes do not count as ten independent confirmations.
3. Build an explicit evidence-to-claim chain. Distinguish observed fact, calculation, model assumption, inference and forecast. Check units, denominators, base rates, selection and survivorship bias, missing samples, revisions and conflicting definitions. Preserve source disagreement instead of averaging incompatible claims.
4. Seek counterevidence deliberately. Compare mechanisms and causal explanations, not merely positive and negative quotations. State the strongest rival explanation, its discriminating observation, missing evidence and the practical consequence of uncertainty.
5. Quantify scenarios and sensitivities when supported. Show assumptions and ranges rather than invented precision. For equity or theme research, connect business exposure to earnings, valuation, expectations already priced, catalysts and invalidation. Historical fit is not predictive proof; out-of-sample validation is required before claimed model-signal utility. Model summaries or sentiment never originate or size trades without accepted validation and the existing trade authority.
6. Synthesize an actionable recommendation or bounded conclusion with confidence calibrated to evidence. Name a falsifier and check-by condition where relevant. More browsing is justified only when it can resolve a material question; neither a first plausible outline nor a large citation count is sufficient.
7. Translate accepted research into the appropriate consumer contract or implementation packet when the parent assignment includes delivery. Preserve lawful data and original implementation; competitor research grants no license to copy code, branding or proprietary corpora.

## Evidence and output

Lead with the decision and reasoning, then decisive evidence, alternatives, assumptions, downside, falsifiers and remaining uncertainty. Include attributable primary references and calculation inputs sufficient to check important claims. Persist material findings through existing research and continuity owners. Report insufficient evidence honestly without calling an untested thesis validated or a research handoff delivered software.
