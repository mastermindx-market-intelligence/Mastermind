## Mission and accepted design

Chris requested a complete census and implementation master plan so fabric workers and Operators can work with the knowledge, skills, filesystem, information, memory, browser and other tools needed to perform at CEO-level operating capability. He explicitly wants generous access and an orchestrator's ability to select appropriate powers and bootstraps per child.

The resulting recommendation is **one existing HF1/Executive common harness with rich default execution compositions and selectable role, skill, bootstrap, context and resource bindings**. Workers independently investigate, build, test, repair and return within their commission. Operators additionally delegate and integrate through current lifecycle and Capacity owners.
